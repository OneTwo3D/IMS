### Reorder Planning draft POs now carry a destination warehouse, delivery dates and a per-row supplier choice (purchasing / UI)

- **New: destination warehouse.** A draft PO generated from Reorder Planning takes the Warehouse filter as its destination. With no warehouse filter it has no destination (never a guessed warehouse) and the toolbar says "Select a warehouse to set the PO destination".
- **New: estimated delivery per line.** Each draft line's expected delivery is the row's Required receipt date, no earlier than today plus the lead time; no Required receipt uses today plus the lead time, so a line never borrows another line's date. The reorder evidence records `expectedDeliveryBasis`. The PO header date is the earliest line date. The reorder evidence on each line records the raw required receipt date, whether it was moved, the Latest PO date and basis.
- **New: supplier override per row.** The Supplier cell is a dropdown of the product's catalog suppliers; the draft line goes to the chosen supplier's draft, flagged in the evidence. A supplier outside the catalog is skipped ("supplier override not in catalog"), never replaced by the default. Overrides clear with the selection on filter or page change. The Reorder rows now expose `supplierOptions`.
- **Fixed: duplicate-draft guard.** The 10-minute double-click guard now also matches on destination warehouse, so reorder runs for two warehouses for one supplier each create a draft. No migration.
