### GR/IR accrual: goods received, not invoiced (accounting / purchasing / Xero)

- **New: a GR/IR sub-ledger.** Every receipt (manual, warehouse book-in and warehouse alignment), supplier bill, bill edit, supplier return and return-generated supplier credit note now writes a signed GR/IR movement in the same transaction as the event, idempotent on the source event. Goods received raise GR/IR at the purchase-order unit cost (goods only, after the order's header discount; freight and landed cost stay on the Stock in Transit path); a bill lowers it by the billed quantity at the same value; a return reverses the receipt portion. A bill whose price differs from the order records the difference as its own **price difference** movement, outside the balance (there was no variance path, so it stays on Stock in Transit as before). It accrues whether or not Xero posting is on.
- **New report: Analytics > GR/IR** (finance and admin roles, CSV export): the open balance by supplier and purchase order, received not billed and billed not received, return credit notes, price difference, and the age of the oldest open movement.
- **New, off by default: GR/IR posting to Xero.** Setting `grir_xero_posting_enabled` and account `xero_grir_account` (Settings > Accounting > Xero). When on, the daily batch posts one reclass journal per date between Stock in Transit and GR/IR (the receipt and bill journals are unchanged, so nothing is counted twice), through the same producer seam and hold as every Xero write; each is also recorded in the transit sub-ledger (`GRIR_RECLASS`) and in the reconciliation pack's transit schedule. While off, nothing is read, staged or posted.
- **Returns and credits agree.** A return-generated supplier credit note clears GR/IR per line at the same order unit cost as the return; the credit's price or FX difference goes to the price-difference movement, so a returned-and-credited line nets to zero. A shadowed (held) journal leaves its movements unconsumed so they post when the hold lifts.
- **Stage tool:** `npm run grir:opening-balance -- --dry-run` derives an opening balance from received-but-unbilled lines that predate the sub-ledger (idempotent; fills the gap between the order lines and the sub-ledger, including lines billed after deployment; no backfill for production).
- **Migration (additive):** new table `grir_subledger_movements` and sync type `DAILY_BATCH_GRIR_RECLASS`. Classified for first load (must be empty); no personal data and no link to a customer or order, so no erasure classification. Help documents: Purchasing, Xero Accounting Sync, Analytics, Glossary.
