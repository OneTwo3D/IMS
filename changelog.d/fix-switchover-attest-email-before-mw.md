### Bridge attestation strictness and storefront e-mail guards before steps M and W (cutover)
- **Bridge attestation (step M).** `writers:check` now requires the report's own `generated_at` **and** the time it was recorded to be at or after the Mintsoft live-from instant (a report taken before it says nothing about the bridge running up to and past it). The unit check requires `UnitFileState` to be exactly `masked` (a `masked-runtime` unit comes back at reboot). `last_trigger: never` is believed only when the report proves the journal was read: `scripts/bridge/bridge_writers_status.py` now prints `journal_exit`, folds the webhook service's start and stop timestamps and every unit's newest journal entry into `last_trigger`, and prints `unknown` (not `never`) when the times were lost and the journal could not be read; `unknown`, and a `never` without `journal_exit: 0`, read as unreadable. The pasted report is documented as the owner's word plus a transcript, not host-signed evidence.
- **Storefront customer e-mail: anchored to the switch-on time.** Turning `storefront_customer_email_enabled` on records `storefront_customer_email_enabled_at` (Settings > Sales and `settings:set`, which now lists the switch). Orders placed before it get no confirmation; dispatched, partial and delivered e-mails go out only for shipments despatched after it; the dispatched e-mail also has a 14-day age bound. With no recorded time nothing is sent.
- **No false "part of your order" e-mail.** The partial-shipment e-mail of an external (warehouse or storefront) dispatch is judged once the whole report has been applied: an order that reached Shipped in the same report gets the dispatched e-mail only.
- **Interlock.** The switch is refused (screen and `settings:set`) unless step W is live and WooCommerce's native processing and completed e-mails read as off; `writers:check` flags the switch on before step W in every phase. `NATIVE_EMAIL_EXCLUSIVE` is now judged per e-mail (each needs exactly one sender).
- **Held orders are confirmed on release.** An order imported pending payment or on hold (a bank transfer) is confirmed once, with the same dedupe, when it moves to processing.
