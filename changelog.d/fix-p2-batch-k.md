### Inventory and costing fixes (purchasing, returns)
- A purchase receipt worth less than half a penny no longer queues a zero-amount stock receipt journal, from either the manual receipt or the WMS book-in.
- A manual purchase receipt now takes the stock row before writing the cost layer, the same order a supplier return uses (the WMS book-in keeps its existing order).
- Restocking a Mintsoft return that no refund expectation covers now books the units at the warehouse's average cost (else the product's historical average) with a cost layer. Units a refund on the same order already restocked (or whose return expectation holds them) are not valued or journalled again: the restock refuses them ("already restocked by the refund; mark the item Refunded only") and only the uncovered remainder is restocked. The value of that remainder is journalled in the same transaction as Dr Inventory / Cr the account of the adjustment reason chosen in the restock dialog (the stock-gain / inventory-adjustment account a manual stock addition uses), not COGS; with no reason account the journal is reported as owed to be posted by hand. A product whose only zero-cost layers are transfer balancing layers or received nothing counts as cost missing; one with a receipt, adjustment, opening-stock or production layer at zero, or a non-inventory product, restocks at zero cost. A product with no usable cost layer is refused with a message to receive stock with a unit cost first.
