### Owner lifecycle review sheet hardened before the real first load (first-load)
- `first-load:prepare` recomputes the owner review set from the run's own inputs (Qoblex Archived, a WooCommerce own or parent status that is not publish, plus the sheet's Mintsoft-discontinued rows): a product that joined the set after the sheet was generated and has no decision is the ERROR `LIFECYCLE_REVIEW_SET_CHANGED` (a warning in a dry run); a decision row for it settles it. `LIFECYCLE_REVIEW_INPUTS_CHANGED` names the figures that are stale.
- The Review tab is protected without a password: only "New IMS status" and "Override reason" can be edited, so a sort or a paste cannot move a decision onto another product. Protected sheets saved by Excel or LibreOffice still read back.
- Archived is refused when any single Qoblex warehouse holds stock (never on the total), and for a VARIABLE parent while any of its variations would load with another lifecycle or holds stock. The sheet shows a parent's variations' stock and highlights it.
- The WooCommerce snapshot (format 3) reads each variation's own stock, so the sheet shows it; format 2 snapshots are refused with a message to re-run `first-load:woo-snapshot`. Generation records are format 2; a format 1 record must be regenerated.
- Stricter reading: a variation published under a draft parent is reviewed; a decided row without a sheet id refuses the decisions; the .xlsx reader refuses duplicate or out-of-order rows and cells, rows or columns beyond the sheet limits, unknown entities, lone surrogates and U+FFFD, local headers that disagree with the directory and parts that inflate past their declared size; the Mintsoft reader reads the product list twice, requires the client and bundle flag on every row, checks stock rows against the list's SKU and bundle flag, checks that the configured warehouses cover every warehouse holding stock, and opens its credentials file without following links.
