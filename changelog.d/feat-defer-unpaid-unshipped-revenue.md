### Defer revenue on unpaid invoiced orders until the goods ship (accounting)
- New ADMIN system switch `xero_defer_unpaid_invoiced_revenue_enabled` (off by default; `npm run settings:set ... --confirm-admin`). With it on, daily-batch Group A1 also defers the unshipped revenue of an UNPAID order whose AUTHORISED invoice IMS posted at or after the switch-on instant (FRS 102 s23.10), dated the invoice's document day, and Group B releases each shipment's share on the day it ships. Same journal, accounts (DR Sales / CR Unearned Revenue) and unshipped-part rules as paid orders. Invoices already open at switch-on stay in Sales (no backfill); DRAFT-mode invoices are never deferred.
- New nullable columns `sales_orders.accounting_revenue_deferral_basis` (PAID / INVOICE) and `accounting_revenue_deferral_event_at` (additive migration, no backfill; NULL reads as PAID). A2, Group B and pre-batch refund adjustments date from the deferral day.
- A payment removed from an invoice-deferred order reopens the receivable with no automatic chargeback credit note; `revenue_posted_without_payment` no longer fires for such orders.
- Switch off: daily-batch output is unchanged.
- Docs: help-docs/xero-sync.md (Daily Batch Sync, new Case 3a) and the stale "manual orders get a DRAFT invoice" statement in Case 3 and the flowchart corrected (the default posting mode sends AUTHORISED).
