### Per-event FX rates for purchasing: step 3, part 2 (returns and supplier credit notes; the interlock is complete)
- Still behind `purchase_event_fx_rates_enabled`, which stays **off by default**; with it off (and never turned on) nothing changes. The built-in interlock is now complete, so the switch can be turned on: that is the owner's decision, after `npm run purchase-fx:reconcile -- --in-flight` and the demo-tenant probe described in Xero Accounting Sync.
- A supplier return takes back from GR/IR the goods cost of the stock layers it consumed, and the order's bills are re-matched to what is still received (returns are subtracted; goods from a positive opening balance count as received).
- Supplier credit notes reverse their bill at the bill's rate; a credit with no bill is valued at the exchange-rate table rate on the day it is posted (refused if missing). A return credit against a bill whose price difference went to Purchase Price Variance is split the same way (not for a reverse-charge bill, which warns).
- Review follow-ups: a shadowed exchange-difference journal is settled and never caught up later; receipts and returns keep reconciling after a switch-off rollback; the remembered prepayment refusal is kept on the order (every process) and yields to a newer read; a booking-in's wait episode is reset once it gets past the rate (or fails otherwise) and the capped warning is written once; `--po` refuses orders the switch never touched; pre-switch bills are measured against their own GR/IR rows; `--in-flight` counts returns and opening balances and flags bills IMS did not post ("no journal": owner decision still open).
- Smaller: the journal of a bill create or edit is dated on the bill date; the re-run's dry run shows the journals it would queue (computed and rolled back); a refused journal no longer holds a confirmed bill deletion; a table-rate bill while Xero sync is on warns; bill lines on an order with a header discount are offered at the discounted cost; a split line with a quantity of more than 4 decimals is refused; bills are named by number; stale "no writer yet" texts corrected.
