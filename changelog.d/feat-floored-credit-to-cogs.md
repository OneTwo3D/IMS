### Supplier credits the stock cannot carry go to cost of sales (purchasing, accounting)
Built, switched off: new ADMIN system switch `landed_cost_floor_cogs_enabled` (default off; `npm run settings:set ... --yes --confirm-admin`). With it off, nothing changes. FRS 102 s13.6.

- **What it fixes.** A negative landed cost line (a supplier credit or rebate) lowers the landed unit cost, floored at 0.00 per PO line. The part the floor cannot absorb was reported (the order page, the receipt result, the `landed_cost_credit_floored` warning) but not posted, and it stayed as a credit on Stock in Transit: the bills put the credit line in transit, and the receipt takes out only the floored value.
- **With the switch on** it is cleared to cost of sales by a COGS_JOURNAL **DR Stock in Transit / CR COGS** at each receipt (manual, WMS book-in, Mintsoft alignment) and each landed-cost recalculation of the order. It is target-state and idempotent: what the floor leaves unabsorbed on the order's receipt layers now, less what this journal already posted; a later freight change posts the difference and a cancelled freight order reverses it. The zero floor and the warning are unchanged.
- **Sub-ledgers.** Each journal writes the matching transit row (`LANDED_COST_CONSUMED_OFFSET`) and COGS row (`LANDED_COST_ADJUSTMENT`), keyed by the journal (prefix `landed-floor-cogs:`), so the transit reconciliation (R11) and the COGS cross-check tie. It goes through the normal accounting queue and producer hold. Not counted twice with the landed-cost revaluation journals (which carry only the absorbed part) or the per-event FX variance (which ignores non-positive cost lines).
- **Refusals.** Turning the switch on is refused while Xero sync is on and the COGS or Stock in Transit account is unmapped. A journal that is owed with an account unmapped refuses a manual receipt or recalculation with a message; a warehouse book-in or alignment is never refused for it.
- **Review round 1.** An order closed short (and one already CLOSED) is worked out on the landed quantities, as its freight is, so the journal neither leaves part of the credit in transit nor over-credits COGS. A layer counts when the transit sub-ledger covers its receipt day (`transit_ledger_coverage_start_date`), so a 0.00 receipt of floored units only is covered; the Mintsoft alignment reconciles for every purchase layer, not only one worth more than 0.00. Callers pass the accounting chart read before their transaction. With the switch off, each event does one read of the switch and one indexed lookup: new index on `transit_subledger_movements (source_ref, source_type)` (migration `20261129120000_transit_subledger_source_ref_idx`, additive, `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY`). Declined, shadowed and (WMS) refused journals are reported after the commit. The floor warning says the amount is posted when the switch is on; turning the switch on also needs `xero_sync_cogs_journal`.
- **Review round 2.** A layer counts only when its receipt journal reached the ledger: a `STOCK_RECEIPT` transit row of the order on its receipt day, and the outbound write hold would have sent that receipt live (not a shadow). This replaces the coverage-date rule: a receipt made with sync off (then sync on) or shadowed under the hold now posts nothing, and no shadow warning is raised for it. A 0.00 receipt of floored units only has no such record and is no longer counted (conservative; the credit stays on transit). Orders already CLOSED when the switch is turned on are out of scope (nothing reconciles a CLOSED order); the close-short basis is used only when the close-short revalues. The chart is now a required argument (no pooled read inside the transaction). The Mintsoft alignment reconciles once per order after all its allocations, and its layers are counted. New migration `20261129121000_transit_subledger_source_ref_idx_validate` checks the CONCURRENTLY index is valid.
