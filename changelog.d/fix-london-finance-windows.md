### Sales, finance, purchasing and production reports on London business days; COGS credit window aligned

- **Changed: the Sales, Customer, Margin (Gross Margin), Returns, Returns by reason, Fulfillment and Throughput report periods are London business days.** A From date starts at London midnight, a To date includes the whole London day (23:00Z to 23:00Z in summer time, midnight to midnight in winter), and the default period is the last 30 London days. Before, a dispatch at 23:15Z on 30 June (London 1 July) and its refund at 23:40Z the same night were split across two months, and the Gross Margin report disagreed with the COGS Report for the same month. Throughput rows are now bucketed by London day. Winter (GMT) results are unchanged.
- **Fixed: the COGS Report's refund-credit window now matches its revenue and cost window.** The credit was loaded over the UTC day while revenue and COGS were London days, so in summer a refund in the boundary hour was not netted against the revenue booked in the same period and the totals looked exact when they were not. Both now use the same London window, which is also the one Gross Margin uses, so the two reports' COGS totals agree for a summer-time month.
- **Fixed: the VAT report, Currency Summary and FX Gain/Loss periods are London days.** Xero documents and VAT tax points are London-dated, so an invoice at 23:30Z on 30 September (00:30 BST on 1 October) was reported in the wrong VAT quarter. The EU VAT shadow report's `--from/--to` order selection uses London days too.
- **Changed: Purchasing reports and the production variance report's date filters are London days;** purchase-order sent/received dates and the Spend report's month buckets show the London day.
- **Fixed: Landed Cost CSV shows the London creation date.** New `createdDate` column (the row's UTC instant is no longer the only date). The Stock Movement Ledger page's Date column shows the London date of the movement, so a movement at 23:30Z on 31 July no longer looks like a filter error inside an August report.
- **Docs.** The previous London-days change also moved the Stock Adjustment report, the Stock Transfer report and the ledger export row count to London days; that was not listed. `help-docs/analytics.md` now lists them and the reports above, and records that Reorder Planning demand, velocity and reorder timing remain on UTC days.
