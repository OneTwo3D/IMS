### Reorder Planning: latest PO date, required receipt and coverage weeks (Analytics)

- **New: Latest PO date and Required receipt on every Reorder Planning row.** Required receipt is the day projected available stock falls to safety stock (to zero when there is none), stepping each dated open purchase order in on its expected delivery; Latest PO date is that day minus the lead time. It is the reorder point restated in dates, so the two cannot disagree. A Latest PO date in the past shows a red **Overdue** badge. Rows with no demand or no run-out within 365 days stay on the list with a dash. Open PO lines with no expected delivery are excluded from the dates and counted in a notice. A PO already past its expected delivery is not received, so it is also excluded and shown as an amber **Late PO** badge; arrivals due today count in today's stock. A row whose Latest PO date is overdue, or that is short today with a late PO as its only cover, stays in the default list even at suggested quantity 0 (the late PO is on order and is not re-ordered), with a note to chase the supplier; no draft is generated for it.
- **New: Order qty and Coverage weeks.** Edit the quantity and the weeks of cover follow, or type the weeks and the quantity follows; each row has a Reset to the system suggestion. Planning only: not saved, and Generate still drafts the system suggestion.
- **Changed: the Reorder Planning CSV** adds Required receipt, Latest PO date, Overdue, timing basis, First shortage date, Late PO quantity and Late PO due date columns. Rows with no supplier now show an amber **Unassigned** tag.
- No schema change.
