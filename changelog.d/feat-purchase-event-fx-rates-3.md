### Per-event FX rates for purchasing: step 3, part 1 (the supplier bill)
- Still behind `purchase_event_fx_rates_enabled` (off by default); with it off nothing changes. The switch still cannot be turned on: supplier returns and credit notes at the bill's rate come in the next part, which flips the interlock. The interlock now also applies on read: while it is not complete the switch reads off whatever is stored.
- A foreign supplier bill is valued and posted to Xero at the order's prepayment lock rate (the exact bank rate of the prepayment recorded in Xero), or, with no prepayment, at the exchange-rate table rate on the bill date (refused if missing). The rate is resolved per bill and stored on it (`fx_rate_source`, `fx_event_rate_to_base`); an edit keeps the bill's own rate.
- Each goods line's stock part stays at the PO cost; any bill-vs-order price difference goes on its own bill line coded to the Purchase Price Variance account, in the supplier's currency, with the line's tax treatment.
- Where the goods were received at another rate than the bill (a table-rate receipt before the prepayment, or table rates on different dates), the difference is cleared per bill by a new GBP journal (sync type `PURCHASE_FX_VARIANCE`): Realised FX gains/losses against Stock in Transit, computed per receipt, adjusted on bill edits and receipts that arrive later, reversed when the bill is deleted or voided. GR/IR is debited at the receipts' value, with the FX and price differences as their own variance rows, so GR/IR and transit net to zero for foreign orders.
- Step-2 review round 2: a "no prepayment" answer stands for an automatic receipt only while it is at most 15 minutes old, and manual receipts and bills always ask Xero (the setting `purchase_fx_no_prepayment_ttl_hours` is retired); the fallback warning says when Xero was asked. The hourly Mintsoft alignment asks about each order at most once per sweep, and remembers a refusal for a while. A booking-in waiting on the purchase rate fails (with a warning) after 10 waits or 3 days; a blank account mapping, or a currency the exchange-rate feed has never held, is no longer treated as "wait". The order's prepayment panel also lists Mintsoft alignment receipts; cost layers record how they were valued. The supplier-not-linked refusal no longer suggests posting a bill.
- Migrations `20261126180000_purchase_fx_variance_sync_type` and `20261126180100_purchase_bill_event_fx` (additive: the sync type, nullable bill / bill line / cost layer columns and a booking-in wait counter; no backfill).
