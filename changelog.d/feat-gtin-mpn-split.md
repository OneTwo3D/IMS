### GTIN / MPN identifier split for Mintsoft and WooCommerce (flag, default OFF)

- **Added: a pure GTIN/MPN classifier.** `isGtin` (digits, length 8/12/13/14, GS1 check digit, fewer than four leading zeros), `stripLikeWooCommerce`, `sameCode` and `classifyMintsoftCodes` are ported from the WooCommerce/Mintsoft sync plugin with identical semantics, so the plugin, the storefront guards and IMS agree on what a GTIN is. A zero-padded counter such as `00000287` is not a GTIN; two part numbers resolve to an MPN plus a reported leftover, never a silent drop.
- **Added: the setting `product_identifier_split_enabled`, default off.** Every changed write sits behind it (Sync > Products > Split product identifiers). With it off the Mintsoft and WooCommerce payloads, the Mintsoft payload hash and the options handed to the connector are byte-identical to before; tests pin this. It is not live.
- **With the setting on, Mintsoft:** the GTIN is written to EAN and the MPN to UPC; Mintsoft's EAN and UPC are read by shape; the single-barcode state machine becomes two slots. A code neither slot can take is raised as a barcode conflict naming every code, and neither Mintsoft field is written while it is open.
- **With the setting on, WooCommerce:** `global_unique_id` is sent only for a real GTIN, and the MPN is written to Yoast's product identifier store (key `mpn`, parent or variation key as appropriate), preserving the other identifiers. An unrecognised store shape is left untouched and recorded on the sync log row.
- **Added: a read-only report** (`scripts/report-identifier-reclassification.ts`) that lists which existing barcodes would move to MPN and which would land in a conflict. It writes nothing. Help documents: Inventory, WooCommerce and Warehouse.
