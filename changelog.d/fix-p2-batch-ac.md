### Order tax and discount fixes (sales, WooCommerce import)
- WooCommerce stores with "Round tax at subtotal" no longer have orders refused at import: the total check allows half a minor unit (not a quarter) only when the reported taxes are visibly unrounded; a whole-unit gap is still refused.
- A manual order's discount can now be taxed at its lines' rates (apportioned with the allocator the order analytics use) instead of the header rate, which also stops a capped discount on a mixed-rate exclusive order driving the header tax negative. It is OFF unless `IMS_APPORTION_ORDER_DISCOUNT_TAX=1`: the invoice still posts the discount on the header rate, so leave it off until posting follows. It applies only to orders created or edited after it is on; stored orders are never re-priced.
