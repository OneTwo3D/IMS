### A payment that Xero holds only in part is now measured and recorded on the order (backend / accounting / sales)

- **New: a sales invoice that loses part of its payment in Xero no longer leaves the order reading as fully paid for ever.** When a payment is deleted or reduced in Xero, the IMS measures the amount from Xero's own figures (what it recorded as paid, less what Xero reports paid), records it on the order, and tells the operator once, with the exact figures and, where it can be matched exactly, the Xero payment that vanished. Before this, the order stayed fully paid and the same warning repeated on every poll and recheck.
- **The order is no longer fully paid, and every reader that asks sees it:** the order list shows **Part paid**; the order page shows the reduced paid amount and the balance due; the receivables reports (AR aging, customer aging, the Invoices tab) include the missing amount from the date it was recorded; the invoice PDF drops its PAID stamp; and the order is not pushed to the warehouse while the shortfall stands. **Paid** itself stays set, so **Mark Paid** is not re-armed over money that already moved.
- **Nothing is posted and revenue is not touched.** No credit note is raised and no deferred or recognised revenue is unwound: crediting the amount or writing it off as a bad debt is the order owner's decision, and the warning says so. A recheck of the same Xero state is idempotent. A further removal adds to the recorded amount, a payment that comes back subtracts, and an invoice that reads paid again clears it. A full removal (nothing paid) is unchanged.
- Orders paid in a shopping channel or marked paid by hand, orders with a credit note or refund, invoices carrying allocated credit, a different currency, and any figure Xero did not state stay withheld exactly as before.
- Additive migration: two nullable columns on `sales_orders` (`payment_removed_foreign`, `payment_removed_at`) and a new history table `payment_removal_reconciliations`. Help document: Xero sync.
