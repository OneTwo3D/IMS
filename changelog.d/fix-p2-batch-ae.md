### Sales credit notes and invoices: allocation, invoice numbers, credit-note history VAT, refund VAT fence (sales / accounting)

- **New, OFF by default: apply a posted sales credit note to its Xero invoice.** With `xero_sales_credit_note_allocation_enabled` set to `true`, a credit note that has just posted as Authorised is allocated to the order's invoice (capped at what the credit note has left and the invoice still owes). Best effort, once, after the post: a refusal is logged as a warning on the order and never fails the credit note. With the setting off nothing changes. Posting of the refund cash-out (paying the customer back) is not part of this change.
- **Fixed: generating an invoice number for an order whose invoice was already queued minted a second number that the accounting sync later overwrote.** The order now takes the number the queued (or posted) invoice carries; with nothing queued a number is minted as before. No posting key changes.
- **Fixed: the credit-note history recomputed VAT and gross from the current tax table, so editing a tax mapping changed what a posted credit note showed.** Each refund line now records its VAT rate when the refund is created (new nullable column `posted_tax_rate`, additive, no backfill) and the history shows that. Older refunds, which have none stored, are still priced from the table and flagged as estimated in the data.
- **Fixed: the refund VAT fence skipped the server-added order-discount reversal on public refunds.** The reversal is now refused when it would post at a rate other than the one the order's discount was charged at, with the reason shown.
