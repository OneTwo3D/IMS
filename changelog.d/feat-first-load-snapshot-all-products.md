First load: the read-only WooCommerce snapshot now reads EVERY product (any type, every status) with its own status, SKU, id, title, type and stock quantity, not only variable products and their variations. It writes a new canonical dataset `woo-product-status.csv` (bound to the verified snapshot like `variant-parents.csv`: byte-identical regeneration, its SHA-256 in the provenance, refused when stale or unbound) and `first-load:prepare` takes the lifecycle of simple, KIT and BOM products from it (private, draft, pending become DRAFT; publish becomes ACTIVE, a Qoblex EOL stays EOL). `LIFECYCLE_NOT_FROM_WOO` now lists only products WooCommerce does not hold. The snapshot format is now 2 (a format-1 snapshot is refused with a message to re-run it), and the operator's `visibilityChecked` attestation gains an `allProducts` key (per-status counts of all products in wp-admin). Also: a SKU repeated with conflicting WooCommerce statuses is refused (`LIFECYCLE_WOO_STATUS_CONFLICT`), the old-CSV message says the CSV predates the `variationStatus` column, and the apply manifest's `wooSnapshot` takes an optional `productStatus` path. Read-only: nothing is written to WooCommerce.
