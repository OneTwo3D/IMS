### Unrealised FX revaluation retired: Xero revalues open foreign balances itself (accounting)
- **IMS posts no unrealised FX revaluation journal any more.** With multi-currency on, Xero revalues open foreign-currency invoices, bills and bank balances itself (its Unrealised Currency Gains and Bank Revaluation system accounts, and the Foreign Currency Gains and Losses report), so an IMS `UNREALISED_FX_JOURNAL` would state the same movement twice, against an AR/AP control account Xero refuses on a manual journal. Owner decision 2026-11-22.
- `/api/cron/accounting-fx-revaluation` still authenticates and rate-limits, then answers `success: true, skipped: true` with a named reason and does nothing: no document read, no rate lookup, no reversal, no enqueue, no activity entry. It is not in the Scheduler; remove it from a server crontab that still calls it.
- An `UNREALISED_FX_JOURNAL` row already queued (a revaluation or the reversal of one) is retired by the Xero processor, not sent: Cancelled with the reason on the row and an `xero_unrealised_fx_journal_retired` warning, before any request, exactly as the realised FX journal already is. The enqueue already suppressed the type for Xero.
- The FX Gain/Loss report stays, as information only (wording updated). The Unrealised FX Gain/Loss account and the Unrealised FX Revaluation switch are no longer used; their Settings descriptions say so, as do the realised ones.
- Docs: help-docs/xero-sync.md (Unrealised FX section, cron table, account mapping: bills post every line to Stock in Transit, there is no Purchases account; rate flow names `fx_rates` behind `fx_rate_source`, Aelia the master and ECB the default), help-docs/sales.md (the stale realised FX section), glossary, settings cron table, analytics, installation checklist, architecture. No migration.
