### Refund delete and Xero document delete: review follow-ups before `refund_delete_enabled` is switched on (sales, accounting)

Both features stay off by default (`refund_delete_enabled`, `xero_document_delete_enabled`). Every change makes IMS refuse, wait or say more, never send more.

- **The void never leaves after its transaction can have rolled back.** The Xero token is refreshed before the refund delete takes its locks and handed to both Xero calls, and each call carries the transaction's deadline: it is aborted at it, and not sent at all when too little of it is left at the last step before the network (new transport refusal `bounded-deadline`).
- **An attempt that sent nothing waits instead of failing.** A lock wait that ran out, too little time, Xero's rate limit, outbound writes held, an unreachable connection: the *Credit note void* entry is handed back (activity `xero_sync_refund_delete_deferred_before_post`, 15 minutes, no retry spent) for up to a day. The entry that uses up its retries raises the operator task `REFUND_DELETE_VOID_STUCK` (additive migration `20261122190000_refund_delete_followups`).
- **A refusal is a terminal state.** When the delete is refused as the void entry runs and no attempt of it can have reached Xero (each void request is now stamped first), the entry is closed (cancelled, proven never sent) with a warning on the order (`refund_delete_void_cancelled`); asking for the delete again queues a fresh void.
- **The books lock date is re-checked** against the credit note's day immediately before the void is sent.
- **Follow-ups survive a crash.** The deletion record notes when the order was re-reserved and the stock change queued (`follow_ups_done_at`, same migration); a void entry retried after a stop in between does them.
- **Operator tasks.** The "voided in Xero, IMS refund stands" ERROR row is written once with its task; both refund-delete tasks close when the refund is finally deleted, and are listed in their own Sync > Exceptions section (*Refund deletes that need you*), not with warehouse queries.
- **Pending-delete fence, more writers.** Checked under the order (or purchase order) lock: the order edit and exchange that queue an invoice update, the bill edit's update producer, posting a drafted supplier credit note (now under the purchase order lock), registering a receipt in Xero (and so the payment-matching pass), the WooCommerce paid flag, and the credit-note allocation (it waits). An invoice or bill update queued after the request now counts as late activity.
- **Shared Xero day budget.** Read for the connected organisation only, and an observation is fresh for 15 minutes (was an hour).
