### Purchase orders: a priced PO PDF, and "Confirm & Send PO" now emails it to the supplier (UI / backend)

- **New: purchase order PDF** at `GET /api/purchase-order/[id]` (Purchasing permission; supplier-portal users are refused) and a **PO PDF** button on every purchase order. It shows supplier, delivery address (destination warehouse), expected delivery, currency, payment terms, lines with barcode, supplier SKU, quantity, unit cost, discount and total, and a totals block (subtotal after discounts, discounts included, VAT, additional costs, total). The Settings > Documents > Purchase Order preview now draws through the same renderer, so preview and real document match.
- **Built, and switched OFF by default: "Confirm & Send PO" can email the supplier.** New setting `purchase_order_email_enabled` (default off; Settings > Company > Documents, administrators only). **Off:** Confirm & Send PO confirms the order and queues nothing (the notice says emailing POs is switched off and to download the PDF and send it yourself) and **Email PO** is hidden. **On:** it confirms first, then queues a `PURCHASE_ORDER` email with the PDF attached on the existing email outbox (the drain delivers it with the installation's SMTP settings and suppression list). A supplier with no (or an invalid) email address, or a failed enqueue, leaves the order confirmed and the page says nothing was sent. **Email PO** re-queues it on a confirmed order; a second click while one is undelivered is not duplicated. The outcome is logged on the order.
- **The drain refuses a PO email while the switch is off,** including rows queued before it was turned off: the row ends FAILED with "Not sent: emailing purchase orders to suppliers is switched off" and nothing is sent.
- **The switch-off is race-safe:** the drain re-checks the flag at the delivery boundary, inside a transaction holding a shared advisory lock through the SMTP send; saving the switch takes the lock exclusively, so it waits for sends already past their check and later sends are refused. The line discount is no longer printed beside a net unit cost (qty x unit cost = line total; discounts are stated once in the totals block).
- Help documents: Purchasing and Documents & Email corrected.
