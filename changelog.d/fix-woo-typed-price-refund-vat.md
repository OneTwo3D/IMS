### Fix: a typed price refund from WooCommerce is no longer refused for missing VAT
- WooCommerce's refund screen fills a goods line's VAT only when its quantity changes, so a price typed on a line (for example 5.00 on a 20% line) arrives with total 5.00 and VAT 0. When such a no-quantity goods line comes with a quantity, shipping or fee line, IMS now reads the typed figure as including VAT at the order's single VAT rate (net 4.1667, VAT 0.8333), so the credit note's VAT is 0.83 and the refund total still matches the storefront.
- When the order's rate cannot be established, or the line's own rate differs from it, the refund is still parked on Sync > Exceptions, with a message that says no VAT was stated (not "map a tax code") and that the money has already been returned. Lines that state VAT, zero-rated lines and EAS/IOSS rate-182 refunds are unchanged.
