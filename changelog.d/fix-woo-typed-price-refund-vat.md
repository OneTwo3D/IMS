### Fix: a typed price refund from WooCommerce is no longer refused for missing VAT
- WooCommerce's refund screen fills a goods line's VAT only when its quantity changes, so a price typed on a line (for example 5.00 on a 20% line) arrives with total 5.00 and VAT 0. When such a no-quantity goods line comes with a quantity, shipping or fee line, IMS now reads the typed figure as including VAT at the order's single VAT rate (net 4.1667, VAT 0.8333), so the credit note's VAT is 0.83 and it matches the storefront refund to within a penny per line.
- When the order's rate cannot be established, the refund is still parked on Sync > Exceptions, with a message that says no VAT was stated (not "map a tax code") and that the money has already been returned. On an EU-scheme order (IOSS, VOEC, DDP) a typed price is still parked for recording by hand. Lines that state VAT and zero-rated lines are unchanged.
