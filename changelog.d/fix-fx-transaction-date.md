### Sales credit-note refund payments: dated and rated on the day the cash went out (switch off by default)
- New system switch `xero_refund_payment_cash_out_date_enabled` (default off; `npm run settings:set`). With it on, a credit-note refund payment whose cash evidence is a refund payment recorded on the order is sent to Xero dated on that payment's Paid date (the latest of several), with the exchange rate for that day from `fx_rates`, instead of the credit note's date and rate. A refund paid days after the credit note is then in the bank on the right day, and Xero books the realised FX at the right rate. Gateway refunds, and a payment dated before the credit note, keep the credit note's date. A missing rate refuses. The credit note's date is kept on the row (`creditNoteDate`); a payment that may already have reached Xero is never re-dated. Off, nothing changes. It only matters while `xero_credit_note_allocation_enabled` is on.
