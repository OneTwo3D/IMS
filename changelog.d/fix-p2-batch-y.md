### Accounting reconciliations and sub-ledgers (accounting)
- A rounding sweep is no longer posted when the net of the posted sweeps of the trailing 30 days would pass £5 (sweeps that alternate in sign net out; failed and cancelled sweeps do not count). A residue held back by that cap is reported as a warning, "recurring small residue not swept (cap reached)", not as a critical gap beyond rounding tolerance.
- The Inventory Valuation GL variance now compares with Inventory plus Allocated Inventory, and is blank when the Allocated Inventory balance for the date is missing.
- A retrospective manufacturing-cost reclass records its COGS leg in the COGS sub-ledger.
- COGS and Transit sub-ledger rows given a timestamp are dated on the London business day, matching their journals.
- The accounting invariant report adds a warning that compares the COGS and Transit sub-ledgers with the journals as queued, independently of the Xero balances. Failed and cancelled journals are reported apart, journals compacted away are counted as unreadable, reclass journals queued before their sub-ledger row existed are left out, and a probable chart re-bind makes the check inconclusive (an info finding) rather than a false warning.
