- **Purchase orders: profit margin per line (internal, read-only).** The PO create/edit form now shows, for users with the `inventory.prices` permission, each line's gross margin % and margin per unit against the product's default list price (ex-VAT, base currency), using the entered unit price converted at the order's exchange rate (plain unit price, not landed cost), plus the product's current cost and this supplier's last price, and an order-level margin over the priced lines. Negative margins are highlighted; a missing exchange rate shows "Rate unavailable" and a line with no sales price shows "—" and is left out of the total. It is never shown to suppliers. No thresholds, blocking or approval yet. Help document: Purchasing.
