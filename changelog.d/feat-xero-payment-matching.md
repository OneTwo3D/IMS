### Automatic customer-payment matching to open Xero invoices, shadow first (OFF by default)

- **Added: a cron-swept payment matching pass** (`lib/domain/accounting/payment-matching.ts`, run by the FX-rates cron in its own error boundary). It finds customer receipts of a Xero-posted order that have no INVOICE_PAYMENT row (so no Xero payment id) and matches each to the open invoice by payment method and currency, through the existing payment-method to bank-account map. It adds no second posting path: the guard is `decideInvoicePaymentRegistration` and a live registration is `registerInvoicePaymentWithLedger`, the same path as Add payment and the receipt sweep.
- **Added: the setting `xero_payment_matching_mode` (off, shadow, live), default off,** changed on the new **Sync > Payment matching** page by an administrator (`sync` permission plus the ADMIN role; every change is logged). Anything other than `shadow` or `live` reads as off.
- **Shadow** records what would be posted (payment, invoice, bank account, amount, amount still open, reason) in the new `xero_payment_matches` table and the page, with zero Xero writes and no sync row. **Live** claims each receipt through that table (one row per payment, a conditional claim, stale claims retaken) and registers it once; a failure is recorded and retried up to five times.
- **Never guessed or forced:** no mapping for the method and currency, or a different currency from the invoice, is *Not matched* with the reason; a receipt over the amount still open on the invoice (invoice currency) is held back; a receipt dated on or before the imported Xero books lock date is held back and its date is not moved; a foreign receipt without its stored rate, and the outbound write hold, hold it back. The pass makes no network call of its own.
- **Migration:** `20261117170000_xero_payment_matches` (one new table and one enum), additive. The table is classified runtime state for the first load and not personal for erasure.
- **Help docs:** `help-docs/xero-sync.md`, new section *Payment matching*.
