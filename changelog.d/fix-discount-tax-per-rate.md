### Order discount tax per line rate: every consumer now follows it, switch stays OFF (sales, Xero, refunds, VAT report)
- With `sales_order_discount_tax_apportion_enabled` ON, a manual order's discount is taxed at its lines' rates and the order stores the split (new column `sales_orders.discount_tax_split`, additive, written only with the switch on, never backfilled). The switch is now registered as an admin system switch (`npm run settings:set`, `--confirm-admin`) and documented in the installation guide; it stays OFF by default, and with it off every write, payload, refund and report is byte for byte what it was. The environment variable `IMS_APPORTION_ORDER_DISCOUNT_TAX` named in an earlier review does not exist any more and nothing reads it.
- The Xero sales invoice (and an update of it) posts the discount as one negative line per tax code on the discount account, so the VAT per rate equals the order's; the invoice reconciliation and the EU scheme re-typing follow the parts, and parts that do not add up to the discount are refused rather than sent.
- A refund of an apportioned order reverses the discount per rate (each rate's share in proportion to the sale net refunded at that rate, the completing refund taking the remainder), each under its own rate's tax code (new column `sales_order_refund_lines.discount_tax_rate_id`); the refund dialog preview is the same cut. An automatic chargeback on such an order is skipped to the manual path until it mirrors the split.
- The VAT report takes each rate's share of an apportioned discount off that rate's row (base and VAT), leaves only the shipping VAT on the order-level row, and nets a per-rate discount reversal on its own rate row, whatever period the order is in. Orders without a split are unchanged.
- The WooCommerce order-create reconciliation (the "round tax at subtotal" tolerance) is unchanged. An attempt to make it exact and per-rate was pulled after review: WooCommerce rounds the grand total once over the unrounded taxes, so a correct version needs that single-rounding formula and the live store's round-at-subtotal and price-decimals settings. It stays open in the follow-up bead.
- A split that no longer adds up to the order's discount (beyond rounding) now refuses the invoice instead of being spread onto one rate (the refusal of an apportioned discount when a line has no tax rate was later narrowed, see `fix-discount-split-pre-enable`).
- Known gap, switch stays off until it is closed or accepted: the reconciliation pack's VAT item still compares IMS line tax per rate without discount VAT on the IMS side (as it already did for header-rate discounts).
