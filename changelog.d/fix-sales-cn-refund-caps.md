### Sales credit-note refund payments can no longer pay out more than was received (xero_credit_note_allocation_enabled, still off by default)
- A refund payment against a sales credit note is now capped at the receipts recorded for the order, held to what Xero shows paid on the invoice (Amount Paid), less the refund cash already paid out, in flight or possibly landed against the order's other refunds. If Xero shows less paid than IMS recorded, or Amount Paid cannot be read, no bank refund is sent and the sync row says why.
- Two refunds of one order are sent under an order-level lock with the cap read again under it, so two refunds processed at the same time can no longer both pay out the same cash.
- A queued settlement that failed before anything was sent is re-capped on retry instead of replaying its old amount; only a row that may already have reached Xero replays its saved request (and is refused if replaying it would now over-pay).
- The unique index that stops two live follow-up rows for the same document now covers `CREDIT_NOTE_ALLOCATION` (additive migration; it refuses to deploy, deleting nothing, if live duplicates already exist, which cannot happen while the switch is off).
