### WooCommerce refunds with an amount-only goods line, and the returns-in-transit switch
- A WooCommerce refund that carries money on a goods line with no quantity beside a quantity line, a refunded shipping line or a refunded fee is now recorded automatically. The amount-only line becomes its own refund line on its order line (quantity 0: no stock movement, no cost reversal; its net and the VAT WooCommerce states are credited, held to what the line has left across all the order's refunds). Before, it was skipped, the refund never reconciled and was retried for ever (with a fee it was parked for a person). A line that cannot be linked to an order line (the order was edited in IMS) or that carries VAT but no net is parked for a person with a message. Amount-only lines alone on a refund keep the existing money-refund path. No migration.
- `refund_returns_in_transit_enabled` is now an ADMIN system switch in `npm run settings:set` / `settings:get` (still off by default, behaviour with it off unchanged). Turning it on is refused while Xero sync is on and the Stock in Transit or Inventory account is unmapped.
- Docs: the recommended WooCommerce returns setup is to enter the refunded quantity and leave "Restock refunded items" unticked (docs/refund-in-transit.md, Sales, WooCommerce).
