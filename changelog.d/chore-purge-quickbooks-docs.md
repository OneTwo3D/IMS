### Retired QuickBooks connector removed from current docs (docs)

- Current docs and help pages no longer mention the retired QuickBooks connector; they describe Xero as the one accounting connector (the design still allows another to be added).
- Historical records are left as they were: `docs/completed/`, `docs/scratch/`, dated plans and decision records under `docs/todo/`, and the connector-removal notes under `docs/archive/`.
- `help-docs/xero-sync.md` names the Sync → Exceptions section for follow-up rows of a retired connector (read and escalate, never settle by hand) and describes Xero's two back-reference conflict paths as they actually work.
