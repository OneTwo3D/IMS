### Condition check on returns: the 3PL write-off route follows the binding's stock mode (inventory)
Advice only; the switch `return_condition_check_enabled` stays off by default and nothing is posted differently.

- An unsellable unit at the 3PL (returns inbox restock, or a refund of units shipped from a WMS-bound warehouse) is still refused; the refusal now gives the write-off route of the binding that is known when it fires (the inbox row's warehouse, or the bound warehouse the refunded units shipped from). **Align To WMS:** restock as Sellable, the 3PL writes the unit off in Mintsoft and the next stock sync's align-down books it under the binding's align-down reason; no IMS stock adjustment (that would write it off twice). **Notification only / disabled:** a real write-off in Mintsoft (no longer "or hold it out of sellable stock") and an IMS stock adjustment. With no binding known (for example only a synced push link), both routes are given. The inbox restock dialog shows the same advice for the item's warehouse.
- The refusal for a quarantine expected warehouse with returns in transit on now says plainly that, with both switches on, an unsellable return is never quarantined: it is restocked as Sellable when the parcel arrives and then written off.
- help-docs: the write-off is valued at FIFO (the warehouse's oldest open layers), not at the returned unit's own layer.
