### Xero unitdp=4 rounding adjustment sized from the whole document, tax included (accounting / Xero)

- **Fixed: a six-decimal unit price could still leave a Xero document a penny or more away from the order.** The "Rounding adjustment" line was sized from the line amounts before tax and posted untaxed, so a tax-exclusive 20% line of 1,000 x 0.333333 (IMS 400.00) came out in Xero as 333.30 + 66.66 + 0.03 = 399.99 and a full payment could not settle it. IMS now predicts the complete document total the way Xero computes it (each line rounded to the penny, tax per line and rounded, for tax-exclusive and tax-inclusive documents, from the stored Xero tax-rate list), sizes the adjustment from the difference to the IMS total (0.04 in the example), posts it untaxed on the Rounding account, and predicts the document again: the write is refused unless it now equals the IMS total. Covers sales invoices, purchase bills, sales credit notes and supplier credit notes. No change for documents whose prices Xero keeps exactly.
- **New refusals (nothing is sent):** the tax rate of a line is not in the stored Xero tax-rate list (or the list is stale, or the type has several components), or the difference is more than four-decimal prices and per-line rounding can explain. The existing "map a Rounding account" refusal is unchanged.
- **Fixed: the EU VAT shadow report no longer fails invoices that need a rounding line as `builder_failed`.** The shadow builder is given the Rounding account and the stored tax rates, and the comparator recognises the adjustment line (kind `rounding`).
