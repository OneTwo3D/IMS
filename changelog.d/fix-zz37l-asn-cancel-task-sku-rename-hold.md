### Mintsoft inbound follow-ups (follow-up to PR #1025 review)
- **Cancelling a transfer's dispatch now leaves a durable task.** Each closed ASN that reached Mintsoft gets one "Ask the warehouse to cancel an ASN" row under Sync > Exceptions > Refused or stalled actions, with the text to paste into a warehouse query. One row per ASN; it counts in the banner, alerts after 24 hours and closes with Done once the warehouse was asked. Previously the instruction was a one-off banner.
- **Order push holds an order that carries a renamed SKU the warehouse does not have yet.** Once product sync has detected the rename (an open "SKU renamed in IMS" discrepancy; until then nothing is known and orders push as before), orders whose line carries any SKU other than the one the warehouse holds are held for the warehouse the order is sent to (new orders parked, changes to pushed orders left due, a rename-only hold is a Needs-a-look item) with a clear reason, and are pushed automatically once the discrepancy closes. Orders placed before the rename keep the old SKU the warehouse knows and push as before. Nothing is dropped. Operators can end a hold that sync can never close with **Ignore: end the order hold** on the Mintsoft dashboard (sync permission, fresh sign-in, reason recorded); archiving a product does not release the hold, and renaming back ends the episode. Done on the ASN cancel request records who pressed it in the activity log.
- Documented: open "SKU renamed" discrepancies must be resolved before rolling back to a build older than the enum value (docs/mintsoft.md).
