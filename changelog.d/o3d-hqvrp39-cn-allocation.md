### Xero: sales credit notes are settled in Xero - allocated to the invoice, or refunded (off by default)
- New system switch `xero_credit_note_allocation_enabled` (default off; set with `npm run settings:set`). With it on, each POSTED sales credit note is settled in Xero once both it and its invoice are posted: the credit note was left open before, so the receivable stayed overstated, a cash refund never left the bank in Xero, and the realised FX on a foreign refund was never booked. Off, nothing is queued and nothing changes.
- The settlement is its own sync row type, `CREDIT_NOTE_ALLOCATION`, with its own claim and its own outbox job, processed by the direct runner and the outbox runner alike. It replaces the approach of #1039, which fenced on a claim the SYNCED write had already released and ran from the direct runner only.
- What it does is decided under the claim, in one transaction under the order lock, from what IMS holds at that moment. An invoice with no receipt behind it (no payment, no registered payment, not marked paid): the credit note is allocated to the invoice. A paid or part-paid invoice: the credit note is not allocated; it is refunded, as a Xero payment against the credit note from the bank or clearing account the original receipt's payment method maps to in the payment account map. A WooCommerce refund is treated as paid back through the shop; a manual refund of a paid invoice waits until its refund payment is recorded, then queues.
- The refund payment carries IMS's settlement rate as its CurrencyRate, so Xero books the realised FX on a foreign refund at IMS's rate, exactly as a receipt does. A missing rate refuses the row (nothing sent) with the usual add-the-rate message.
- Retries cannot allocate or refund twice: the decision is pinned on the row before anything is sent, the Idempotency-Key is the row's own, the ledger is checked first on a retry, and a retry that would now decide the other kind of settlement refuses instead.
- Xeroom owns sales credit notes in P1 and P2, so these rows follow the same producer hold as the credit note: shadowed (recorded, nothing sent) while Xeroom owns them.
