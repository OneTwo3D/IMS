### Xero: sales credit notes are settled in Xero - allocated to the invoice, or refunded (off by default)
- New system switch `xero_credit_note_allocation_enabled` (default off; set with `npm run settings:set`). With it on, each POSTED sales credit note is settled in Xero once both it and its invoice are posted: the credit note was left open before, so the receivable stayed overstated, a cash refund never left the bank in Xero, and the realised FX on a foreign refund was never booked. Off, nothing is queued and nothing changes.
- The settlement is its own sync row type, `CREDIT_NOTE_ALLOCATION`, with its own claim and its own outbox job, processed by the direct runner and the outbox runner alike. It replaces the approach of #1039, which fenced on a claim the SYNCED write had already released and ran from the direct runner only.
- What it does is decided under the claim, in one transaction under the order lock, from what IMS holds at that moment. An invoice with no receipt behind it (no payment, no registered payment, not marked paid): the credit note is allocated to the invoice. A paid or part-paid invoice: the credit note is not allocated; it is refunded, as a Xero payment against the credit note from the bank or clearing account the original receipt's payment method maps to in the payment account map, and only when IMS holds evidence that cash left: a refund payment recorded on the order, or a WooCommerce refund that WooCommerce flags as paid back through the gateway (`refunded_payment`, now stored on the refund as `refunded_via_gateway`, additive nullable column).
- A WooCommerce refund made manually (a store-credit coupon, a payment outside the gateway), and any WooCommerce refund imported before the flag was kept, posts NO bank refund and is not allocated to the paid invoice: the credit note stays open in Xero as the customer's credit and IMS logs one warning per refund asking the operator to apply or refund it. A manual refund of a paid invoice with no recorded refund payment waits for one.
- Every refund payment is capped at the smaller of the credit left on the credit note and the receipts recorded on the order less refund payments already made (a 10 receipt with a 50 refund pays out at most 10). When the refund is smaller than the credit left (a part-paid invoice, or the cap), the rest stays open on the credit note and IMS logs a warning with both figures; it does not split one credit note between a payment and an allocation, so the operator applies the rest in Xero.
- The refund payment carries IMS's settlement rate as its CurrencyRate, so Xero books the realised FX on a foreign refund at IMS's rate, exactly as a receipt does. A missing rate refuses the row (nothing sent). The decision (operation, amount, bank account, payment method, rate) is pinned on the row, so a retry sends the same payment.
- Retries cannot allocate or refund twice: the Idempotency-Key is the row's own, the ledger is checked first on a retry, a row that has been attempted is never retired as "not sent", and a retry that would now decide the other kind of settlement refuses instead. A settlement with nothing to apply is retired rather than marked posted.
- Xeroom owns sales credit notes in P1 and P2, so these rows follow the same producer hold as the credit note: shadowed (recorded, nothing sent) while Xeroom owns them, but only while the producer hold is enforced for xero; without the hold they are live like the credit note itself.
