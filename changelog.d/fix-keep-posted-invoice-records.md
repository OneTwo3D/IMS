### Fix: posted invoice tax codes outlive sync-log retention, and a refund that falls back to current codes is flagged (sales)
- Sync-log retention no longer drops the posted tax codes of an order's confirmed sales invoice and invoice-update rows. They are never deleted (they were already never deleted), and at the normal window their payload is now compacted to a projection: the posted tax codes and the rate each was read from, ids, dates, currency, amounts and line quantities stay; customer names, emails, line text and the reference are removed. Every other sales invoice / update row retention compacts keeps the document id it names, so a compacted newer update cannot let an older version be read. Other rows keep today's retention.
- A refund of an invoiced order that has to read the tax rates' current codes for some line (no readable posted record, an update still in flight, or a line that names no rate) writes one activity WARNING on the order, `refund_used_current_tax_codes`, naming the lines, the codes used and why. The refund is not refused.
