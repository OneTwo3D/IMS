### Fix: a refund reverses a posted invoice on the tax codes it was posted with (sales)
- Once a sales invoice is posted, its refunds and credit notes now reverse its goods lines, and the per-rate discount parts of an apportioned order discount, on the accounting tax codes the invoice was posted with (read from the order's latest synced invoice record), not on the rate's current mapping. A tax rate re-mapped to another code after posting only affects invoices not yet posted, so a credit note can no longer move VAT between codes or VAT boxes. The posted-VAT fence, the manual (quarantined WooCommerce) refund recording and the credit-note fallback for lines without a snapshot read the same posted codes. A refund before any posting, and an invoice with no readable posted record (posted by Xeroom or linked by hand), still read the rate's current code.
- This changes a live path: the goods lines of any refund of a posted invoice (switch on or off); the discount path with `sales_order_discount_tax_apportion_enabled` off is unchanged. Shipping, lines with no rate and the header-rate discount still read the order default rate's current code (follow-up).
