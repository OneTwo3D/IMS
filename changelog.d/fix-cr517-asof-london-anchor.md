### Inventory as-of reads anchor on the London business day (inventory)

- The on-hand as-of reader now uses each snapshot's own boundary (the run's stored `cutoffAt`, else the end of its London label day) instead of a UTC midnight. In BST, movements between 23:00Z and 24:00Z on a snapshot day are no longer missed by forward replay or wrongly undone by reverse replay.
- A date-only as-of names a London business day and ends at its London midnight; "today" and the future check use the London day. A precise instant inside a snapshot day anchors on the previous snapshot.
- The negative-stock report's movement window and default dates, and the inventory valuation report's default date, use the same London day. GMT results are unchanged.
- An anchor snapshot whose cutoff is later than the as-of bound (a backfilled summer-time day, or a legacy BACKFILLED run stamped with a UTC cutoff) now has the movements in between reversed out instead of staying in, so the negative-stock and valuation reports no longer double-count that hour. The snapshot backfill writer now stamps London cutoffs and walks London day boundaries; stored UTC cutoffs of legacy backfilled runs are still read correctly. Legacy summer-time CRON runs written before the London label (their stored cutoff is 23h before their content end) are an accepted limit and are not corrected.
- A date-only as-of is reported as the last millisecond of the London day (22:59:59.999Z in BST); the reservation-snapshot lookup follows it.
