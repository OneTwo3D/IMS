### Per-event FX rates for purchasing: the one-off journal for bills posted outside IMS is measured on the Xero bill; switch-on waits for IMS to write bills
- Still behind `purchase_event_fx_rates_enabled` (off) and its interlock (still off); with the switch off nothing changes.
- `npm run purchase-fx:non-ims-bills` reads each candidate's Xero bill once, read-only, and shows the basis it assumes (quantity, goods value in transit, the Xero rate, the account), why IMS has no transit row, and the goods not received yet a confirmation also covers. The journal is measured at the Xero bill's own rate; a bill that is not AUTHORISED or PAID in Xero, whose Xero lines are not all on Stock in Transit, or whose net differs, is not confirmable. A run reads at most `--max-reads` bills (default 50), only the named ones with `--bill`, and stops at the first rate-limited read; it prints how many it read.
- Bills with no Xero link are not candidates; a pending delete or a declined-and-handled posting leaves a bill out unless it is named. Confirming needs `--all` with `--digest`, or `--bill` with `--expect <id>=<amount>` for those bills; the journal is recomputed under the order lock and the order is rolled back on any difference. Unknown or malformed arguments are refused. While the switch is off the list is shown without reading Xero, "not confirmable yet".
- Turning the switch on is refused while the producer-side hold shadows supplier bills (another system writes them, phase P1).
- Smaller: a reverse-charge credit line that is all price variance sends no zero stock line (its VAT penny goes on the PPV line); the price-variance residue warning is written inside the post's transaction; the proposal uses the real Xero sync setting; counts are limited to the confirmed bills; one order's failure no longer stops the others.
