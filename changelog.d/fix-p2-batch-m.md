### Webhook origin, SMTP password at rest and lost tracking pushes (WooCommerce, installer)

- **Fixed: a WooCommerce webhook naming another store in the unsigned `X-WC-Webhook-Source` header is held, not imported.** When the signed body carries neither a self-link nor a permalink (core order bodies never do), the header is the only store identity: a delivery whose signature verifies and whose header matches the bound store is accepted, while a mismatched header is held (503, retried, then Sync > Exceptions) for orders, refunds and products alike. A signed self-link or permalink still wins over the header.
- **Fixed: the installer no longer stores the SMTP password in plaintext.** `provision-instance` writes `email_smtp_pass` as ciphertext when `SETTINGS_ENCRYPTION_KEY` is available (the installer now passes it to the script), and warns when it is not.
- **New, off by default: tracking reconcile sweep (`wc_tracking_reconcile_enabled`).** Re-pushes WooCommerce tracking that was lost between a shipment's commit and its post-commit push. Goes through the outbound hold, leaves completed, cancelled, refunded, failed and trashed WooCommerce orders alone, and does nothing until the setting is `true`. Help document: WooCommerce. Coverage is judged on the whole outbound item (number, carrier and shipped date), so a carrier or date edit whose push was lost is retried too.
