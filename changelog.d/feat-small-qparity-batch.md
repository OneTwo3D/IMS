### Purchase order warehouse flag, supplier-grouped reorder view, barcode and supplier SKU on PO lines (purchasing, analytics, settings)

- **Added: "Available in Purchase Orders" on warehouses.** One boolean on `Warehouse` (`availableForPurchaseOrders`, default true, additive migration `20261117140000_warehouse_available_for_purchase_orders`), so every existing warehouse is unchanged. A warehouse with it off is left out of the purchase order destination list and the Receive Goods warehouse list, is marked **Restricted** in Settings > Inventory > Warehouses, and is refused on the server wherever a PO destination or receipt location is set: creating and editing a purchase order (including CSV import), draft POs generated from Reorder Planning, and `receivePurchaseOrder`.
- **Existing orders keep working.** A purchase order that already points at a restricted warehouse can still be edited with that destination unchanged and can still be received into it; the receipt succeeds with a warning. Receiving into any other restricted warehouse is refused.
- **WMS-driven receipts are not affected.** ASN book-ins come from the warehouse system, which decides where goods land; the flag is never applied to them.
- **Added: Group by supplier on Reorder Planning.** Each supplier group lists its rows with the total recommended quantity; every row keeps its own lead time and latest PO date; rows with no supplier sit in a separate warning group; manufactured products get their own groups; groups recalculate with the warehouse filter. A view only: draft PO generation, the CSV and the selection boxes are unchanged. The grouped view loads every matching row rather than one page.
- **Added: Barcode and Supplier SKU columns on the PO detail page** (Order Lines), with a Columns toggle remembered in the browser. Barcode is added to the PO line select (empty when the product has none); Supplier SKU comes from the existing `getSupplierLastPrices` supplier-product mapping. The supplier PO PDF is unchanged.
- Help documents: Settings, Warehouse (Mintsoft), Purchasing, Analytics.
