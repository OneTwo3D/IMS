### Reorder Planning: "Ignore stockouts" stops out-of-stock days understating demand, and the demand chart shows stockouts (analytics)

- **New: an Ignore stockouts box on Reorder Planning (off by default).** Ticked, each day a product had no on-hand stock is removed from the demand window before averaging (units sold / (window days - stockout days)), so a product that sold out for most of the window is no longer under-ordered. Stockout days come from the daily inventory snapshots, with no new table and no replay of stock movements: one query per report. A day with no snapshot is unknown, never a stockout and never in stock, and stays in the average; the page discloses how many window days the snapshots cover and since when. A product out of stock for 90% or more of the window keeps its unadjusted average. Adjusted rows are labelled under Daily demand. The setting flows through the page address, the CSV export, the demand chart and the draft PO and production order buttons, so drafts use the demand the operator saw. Unticked, reports, CSVs and drafts are unchanged. Help document: Analytics.
- **New: the demand chart shows stock history and stockouts.** Past weeks carry an on-hand stock line from the daily snapshots and red (stockout), dark grey (unknown) and light grey (partly unknown) week shading, with per-week day counts in the data table. This was left out of the first version of the chart.
