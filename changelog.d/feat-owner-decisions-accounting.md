
### Accounting

- **Reverse-charge bills: a net-payable figure.** The stored VAT and totals are unchanged (the VAT return needs them), but what is owed to the supplier is now the stored total less the VAT the bill accounts for itself on its reverse-charge lines. Mark as paid offers it by default, a bill's paid or part-paid status is judged against it, the unrealised exchange-rate revaluation uses it for the open supplier balance, the freight cancellation credit-note gate offsets credit notes against it, and the draft credit note raised for returned goods is grossed up to and limited to it. A bill without a reverse-charge line is unchanged.
- **Rounding line to the order total, behind `xero_round_to_order_total_enabled` (off by default).** With it on, a sales invoice raised from an order carries the order total and its single Rounding adjustment line is sized so the document totals exactly the order total, within the order reconciliation tolerance (never more than 1.00; beyond it the document is refused and nothing is sent). With it off nothing changes. The posting key of a document is the same with the setting on or off.
- **A partial payment removal booked as a credit note, behind `xero_partial_payment_removal_credit_note_enabled` (off by default).** With it on, a payment partly removed in Xero is credited by a credit note for the removed amount through the ordinary refund path (behind the outbound write hold), the VAT and deferred revenue apportioned pro rata (an order-level discount is mirrored as its own line, so the credit note comes to exactly the removed amount). One credit note per removal, claimed under the order lock: the order carrying any other refund leaves it to a person, and a later WooCommerce or operator refund on an order with a removal credit note is refused as a possible double credit (a conflict, like the chargeback's). Only removals detected after the setting was turned on are credited; older ones are listed in an operator report. A refusal that never clears is a final manual-handling state and never holds the payment poll cursor; the credit notes are raised once per poll, after the checkpoint. A payment restored before the credit note is released withdraws the unsent credit note. With it off, a removal is recorded and left to the order owner, as before. Adds a nullable column `refund_id` to `payment_removal_reconciliations` and a unique index on it (additive migrations).
- **Reverse-charge bills: reports and the pay dialog use the net payable.** AP aging and the FX gain/loss report count a reverse-charge bill at its net payable, and the Mark Bill as Paid dialog and the bill row show it (with the bill total as secondary text).
- **Rounding to the order total also covers credit notes.** With `xero_round_to_order_total_enabled` on, a full-refund or chargeback credit note carries the same Rounding line as the invoice it reverses, so it totals what the invoice does.
