### Mintsoft suppliers: mapping screen and the supplier on ASN create (Mintsoft)
- New **Integrations > Mintsoft > Mintsoft suppliers** screen (managers and administrators): a read-only, paced refresh of Mintsoft's supplier list, suggested matches per IMS supplier (case, punctuation and legal-suffix folding, similarity, shared e-mail/VAT/code), and audited decisions: confirm a suggestion, pick any Mintsoft supplier, "create in Mintsoft", unmap. IMS never overwrites an existing Mintsoft supplier.
- "Create in Mintsoft" is a real write (ownership-map row `mintsoft/supplier.create`, step M, producer seam and outbound hold) behind `mintsoft_supplier_create_enabled` (default OFF; off records the choice as pending and sends nothing).
- Transfer ASNs carry the supplier named by `mintsoft_transfer_supplier_id` ("One Two Enterprises Ltd"), mapped through the same screen.
- ASN create sends the mapped supplier id (`NewASN.ProductSupplierId`, UNVERIFIED against the live tenant; controlled-run step in docs/mintsoft.md) ONLY behind its own switch `mintsoft_asn_send_supplier_enabled` (default OFF; ADMIN with a fresh sign-in; ON only after controlled-run step 2). With it OFF the ASN body is byte-identical even when suppliers are mapped. `mintsoft_asn_supplier_required` cannot be saved ON without it; with it ON an unmapped, missing or inactive supplier refuses the ASN and raises a Sync > Exceptions task. Both ship OFF, and both must be ON before step M.
- Safety: a create in Mintsoft needs a list read within `mintsoft_supplier_cache_max_age_minutes` (default 30) and is refused when a similar supplier exists unless "create anyway" with a reason; a CREATING row cannot be unmapped, remapped or re-created, only resolved by an administrator (reason, fresh sign-in); inactive and vanished suppliers are never suggested, mapped or sent; region words and two-letter forms no longer fold as legal suffixes; claims, refusals and unknown outcomes are audited; the read sync is single-flight (advisory lock) and batched; one Mintsoft supplier mapped to several IMS suppliers is warned about.
- Migration `20261119190000_mintsoft_supplier_mapping` (additive): `mintsoft_suppliers` (read cache) and `mintsoft_supplier_links`.
