### Accounting audit: removed Xero payments and completed-order manufacturing cost edits (backend / sync)

- **Fixed: removing a payment in Xero to re-apply it no longer raises a chargeback.** The payment poller treated an invoice that read zero paid as a reversal at once and raised a chargeback credit note. An invoice with nothing paid whose own Xero revision stamp is under 30 minutes old is now withheld (paid flag kept, no credit note) on the existing hourly recheck lifecycle, and admitted as a reversal only if it is still unpaid at the recheck. A payment that is back closes the warning with nothing raised. Voided invoices and bills are unchanged. No migration.
- **Fixed: a manufacturing cost edit on a completed order split COGS wrongly.** Units transferred to another warehouse were counted as sold (10 units, 6 transferred, +10 overhead put 6 into COGS instead of Inventory), and an order with a journaled sold shipment was refused with "rounding produced an unbalanced reclass journal". The COGS leg now covers sold units only (transferred, returned and re-used units are excluded, as in the landed-cost revaluation); the Inventory leg takes the rest, including the part the shipment's COGS revaluation credits to Inventory, and is balanced against the overhead credit at the rounded level. The edit now also propagates into the layers hanging off the output (transfer destinations, later production runs that used the units as a component), through the landed-cost revaluation path under its scope lock: their unit cost, snapshots and already-sold shipment COGS are revalued, and the COGS they still owe joins the reclass COGS leg. This supersedes the downstream-layer part of the audit finding. Help documents: Xero Sync, Manufacturing.
