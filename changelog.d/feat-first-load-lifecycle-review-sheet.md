### Owner lifecycle review sheet and lifecycle decisions for the first load (first-load)
- New read-only command `npm run first-load:lifecycle-review -- --manifest <run.json> --out <dir> [--mintsoft-env-file <file>]` writes `lifecycle-review.xlsx`: every product and variation that is draft, private or pending in WooCommerce, Archived in Qoblex or discontinued in Mintsoft, with its SKU in Qoblex, WooCommerce (with id), Mintsoft (with product id) and IMS (after sku-renames), the WooCommerce name (the Qoblex name, marked, when WooCommerce does not hold it), type and parent, the Qoblex state, the WooCommerce status and parent status, the Mintsoft status, the stock in Qoblex (total and per warehouse), WooCommerce and Mintsoft, and the lifecycle the first load would set; an empty "New IMS status" column restricted to Discontinued, Archived, Do not sell yet and Active, and an "Override reason". Rows holding stock and SKU mismatches are highlighted; an "All products" tab and a "SKU mismatches" tab cover the exact-SKU mapping across the four systems. Every cell is formula-safe; the files are mode 600; product data only.
- Mintsoft is read with GET requests only through the reconciliation pack's guarded reader, from a mode-600 credentials file with the fixed key; without it the Mintsoft columns read "not read" and an Archived decision always needs an override reason.
- New dataset `lifecycle-decisions`, read back from the filled sheet (.xlsx) or its CSV export, with the HIGHEST lifecycle precedence (over the WooCommerce-derived and the Qoblex lifecycle). Each applied decision is reported; an unknown SKU, a bad status, a duplicate and Archived for a product holding stock (or whose stock was not read) without an override reason are refused; a review-set product left undecided blocks a real `first-load:prepare` and is a warning in a dry run. The decisions are bound to one sheet generation (manifest `lifecycleReview.generation`, a sheet id on every row and a hidden binding tab).
- First-load lifecycle follow-ups: a NON_INVENTORY product's conflicting WooCommerce statuses now block like a simple product's (one predicate for "takes its own Woo status"); clearer `LIFECYCLE_NOT_FROM_WOO`, `LIFECYCLE_WOO_STATUS_UNUSABLE` and `LIFECYCLE_WOO_STATUS_CONFLICT` messages; the variation count SQL and the Polylang wp-admin guidance in docs/first-load-input-spec.md corrected.
