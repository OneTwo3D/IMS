### First load: Qoblex State, row selection and owner decision lists (operator tooling)
- Qoblex State `Archived` now loads as a DRAFT product (not end of life; Qoblex archives a product while it is a WooCommerce draft), `Active` as ACTIVE, `Inactive` as EOL, and `Deleted` is skipped from products and stock lots. A purchase order line on a DRAFT product is accepted by the prepare check (same rule as `isPurchasableProductStatus`). A VARIABLE product whose Qoblex lifecycle disagrees with its WooCommerce status is reported as the warning `LIFECYCLE_DISAGREES_WITH_WOO`.
- A column map's `rowSelect` may now be a list of selectors that must all pass (the old single object still works). New optional map keys `nameColumn` and `nonStock`.
- New canonical decision datasets: `non-stock-skus` (load as NON_INVENTORY whatever the source type), `sku-name-overrides` (SKU for a blank-SKU row by product name; without it the row is refused as `BLANK_SKU` naming the product), `supplier-overrides` (currency / purchase tax type for a supplier whose contact row leaves them blank) and `recipe-overrides` (a complete recipe that replaces the export's lines for a KIT or BOM).
- The Qoblex stock report column order changed; the native map's `expectedHeaders` and the fixture follow it. The contact export is documented as suppliers only.
