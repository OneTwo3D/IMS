### Delete a refund recorded in error, with the credit note voided in Xero (sales / accounting)

- **New: a refund recorded in IMS by mistake can be deleted, and the delete undoes exactly what the refund did.** On the order page, Refunds shows **Delete refund** (with a confirmation and an optional reason) when the refund can be deleted, or the reason it cannot. It is **off by default**: switch it on with the `refund_delete_enabled` setting. It needs the same `sales.refund` permission as recording a refund.
- **Everything is unwound from the record of what the refund posted, never recalculated.** Returned stock and the cost layers that return created are removed at the recorded quantity; the COGS reversal is cancelled if unsent or countered by a swapped journal if posted, and the COGS sub-ledger gets the exact opposite movement so it nets back to the pre-refund state; the order is re-reserved and its refund status recalculated. The result therefore does not depend on how refund COGS reversal is valued (the open finance decision, onetwo3d-ims-6oyu.5).
- **The credit note is voided in Xero through the outbox** (new sync type `CREDIT_NOTE_VOID`): idempotent and resumable (the void reads the document first; an already voided one is a success), refused if the credit note was allocated or paid in Xero, never sent while outbound writes are held. A credit note that never left IMS is simply cancelled.
- **Refused, with plain operator wording and no change:** a refund that came from WooCommerce or was raised automatically; a credit note with payments or allocations; a date on or before the Xero lock date; returned stock since used, reserved or moved (or a return IMS did not tag); an order with store credit or an erased order; accounting in flight or unprovable; goods in transit, unearned revenue, allocated inventory or a daily-batch adjustment.
- The whole delete runs in one transaction under the order lock, so it is serialised against a second refund and a shipment on the same order. A tombstone (`sales_order_refund_deletions`) keeps who, when, why and what was reversed, and makes a retry report success without doing anything twice.
- Additive migration: the `CREDIT_NOTE_VOID` sync type, the `sales_order_refund_deletions` table and a nullable `cost_layers.return_movement_id` (set for new return layers only; a return made before this change cannot be reversed and is refused). Help documents: Sales (Refunds), Xero sync.
