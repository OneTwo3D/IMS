### Switchover prerequisites: bridge-units attestation and IMS customer e-mail for storefront orders (cutover)
- **Bridge host attestation** (the evidence `BRIDGE_ATTESTATION`, which used to answer "not built"). New `writers:attest` kind `bridge-units-masked`: `npm run writers:attest -- --kind bridge-units-masked --by <name> --output-file <path|-> [--yes]`, or paste the output on **Sync > Writer confirmations** (administrator, recent sign-in). It records the output of `scripts/bridge/bridge_writers_status.py` (LoadState, ActiveState and UnitFileState of the sweep timer, the sweep service and the webhook service, plus the last trigger time), parsed strictly and refused when older than an hour. From step M `writers:check` requires all three units masked and inactive and the last trigger before the Mintsoft live-from instant; before step M nothing is due. The script lives in IMS and is copied to the bridge host by ops; the bridge repository is not changed here.
- **Customer e-mail for storefront orders from IMS**, behind the new setting `storefront_customer_email_enabled` (default **off**): order confirmation, dispatched, partial shipment (one per shipment) and delivered. Single send per order (per shipment for partial), unknown send outcomes are terminal and shown on the order, the switch is read at queue and at send time. The existing dispatch e-mail skips a storefront order exactly as before unless the new switch is on.
- **`writers:check` in P2-W** reads WooCommerce's native processing and completed e-mail settings (two read-only GETs, only from step W) against the new switch: both on, both off, or an unreadable store fail the check.
- **Storefront customer e-mail: a queue failure is recorded on the order.** A confirmation, partial-shipment or delivered e-mail that could not be queued still never fails the import, despatch or status change, and now also writes a WARNING to the order's activity (it was logged only). Only a later run of the same step queues it again; otherwise the customer misses that one e-mail and an operator can send it by hand.
