### Refund accounting: remainder shares and cancel with a posted invoice (sales / accounting)

- **Fixed: a refund that reaches 99.9% (classed full) left the uncovered unearned revenue in the Unearned account for ever.** A full refund's unearned-revenue reversal now takes whatever is still deferred, not only the value of the refunded lines (a full refund is excluded from every later daily batch, so nothing else would release it).
- **Fixed: refunding the unshipped remainder of a part-shipped order left the shipping/discount share in Unearned.** Once a refund leaves no line with units still to ship, its unearned-revenue reversal releases the rest of the deferral. No posting key changes; only the amount on the existing reversal journal.
- **Changed: cancelling by hand an order whose invoice is posted to the accounting system is refused** until the order has been refunded in full (the credit note is the reversal). The check also covers an invoice an operator marked as posted and an invoice raised by the incumbent invoicing plugin. A cancellation driven by the store, or by an approved EU withdrawal, still goes through: it writes a `cancelled_with_posted_invoice` warning and raises a durable operator task on the order (Sync > Exceptions) saying the posted invoice must be credited by hand, because a cancelled order cannot be refunded. Nothing is posted to the accounting system by the cancellation.
- **Fixed: the remainder sweep could debit Unearned below zero once an earlier refund's journal had been deleted by retention.** Each refund now records the unearned revenue its own reversal debits (new column, additive migration, no backfill); the sweep nets earlier refunds from that figure and never takes more than the order's deferral less its recognition. An earlier refund whose reversal cannot be established (written before the column, journal gone) withholds the sweep and writes a warning on the order. A refund's staging and the daily batch's shipment pass now take the same order row lock, so the sweep cannot debit the same remainder as a shipment journal in flight.
- **Fixed: the shipping/discount share stayed deferred when the refunded unshipped remainder sat on a line that was partly shipped.** "Nothing left to ship" is now judged per line from shipped, ordered and refunded quantities; a refund that brings no stock back is booked against the units that never shipped. Service (non-inventory) lines no longer count as waiting to ship.
- Manual refund totals were already derived server-side from the stored order (sale-line nets cut under the order lock, shipping base derived from its foreign amount, foreign ceiling checked): no change needed.
