### Per-event FX rates for purchasing: what must hold before the interlock release
- Still behind `purchase_event_fx_rates_enabled` (off) and its interlock (still off); with the switch off nothing changes.
- The bill-writer check (the switch cannot be turned on while the producer-side hold shadows supplier bills) is judged on the installation env file (`settings:set ... --env-file <file>`), and refused when the hold's variable is in neither the file nor the shell. It now also applies on every bill's own date, judged as the bill's posting is (the start of the day): while bills are shadowed a new bill stays at the PO rate and a bill valued under the switch is not edited (its stored and its new date are both judged).
- The one-off journal for bills posted outside IMS: a confirmation is refused up front while the hold shadows its journal (it would be lost), and a declined journal is no longer refused as a mismatch; the basis is frozen with the confirmation and goes stale (journal held, bill listed again) when the IMS bill or the order's cost is edited or the bill is re-linked; an edit in Xero needs a re-confirmation (`--bill`, replaces the basis). The confirmation re-checks the bill under the lock and that the connected Xero organisation is the one that was read.
