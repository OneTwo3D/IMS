### Merge holds in Mintsoft (fulfilment, behind a setting)

- IMS can now hold every order of a same-customer group in Mintsoft (awaiting confirmation) so they can be merged there, instead of only flagging them. It is behind the setting `wms_merge_hold_enabled`, which is off by default; with it off nothing changes. It also needs merge candidates on Flag.
  - IMS never merges and never releases a hold: a person merges and confirms the order in Mintsoft. There is no release code.
  - The hold is recorded in IMS, not as a Mintsoft or WooCommerce note. One task under Exceptions > Raise a warehouse query names the held orders (and any that could not be held) with the EU and FedEx warnings.
  - A hold that fails is shown as "Not held" and retried when the failure was a connection problem, not when Mintsoft refused it.
  - After 4 hours (`MERGE_HOLD_REMIND_HOURS`) a hold that is still in place raises one overdue task, a warning in the activity log and a notification, and the order badge turns red.
  - While an order is held, IMS sends no change, cancel or withdrawal hold to Mintsoft for it: a change shows as "Act in Mintsoft" under Exceptions, and a withdrawal or cancel raises a task for a person.
  - The hold clears when Mintsoft's status leaves awaiting confirmation, when a merge is detected, or when the order despatches.
  - A hold request whose answer is lost (timeout, server error, unreadable reply) is no longer treated as "Not held". The order shows "Merge hold unconfirmed" (it may be held), a task names it, and IMS reads its Mintsoft status to settle it: awaiting confirmation means held, NEW means it is retried, anything else stays unconfirmed and raises a task. The merge candidate task stays open meanwhile.
  - If a person confirms the orders in Mintsoft without merging them (or marks the merge candidate task Done), IMS does not hold that same group again. A group whose orders change is treated as new.
  - A Mintsoft status IMS does not recognise (or cannot read) never ends a hold on record: IMS keeps it and raises a task. Every hold state change is written to the order's activity log.
  - A hold whose answer was lost is settled by the Mintsoft status: a status past NEW (allocated, picking, packed, cancelled in Mintsoft) proves it is not held, so the row is settled "not held" and IMS pushes again; NEW only counts on a read taken at least 10 minutes after the attempt (a request that finished late may still land). An unrecognised status raises one task per order. While a hold may be in place, a cancel, withdrawal or change IMS withholds raises a task (or "Act in Mintsoft") at once, the overdue reminder covers it, and Mintsoft is still read after IMS cancels the order. A person's confirm of one order of the group also covers an unconfirmed sibling. The hold request has a 3 minute deadline.
