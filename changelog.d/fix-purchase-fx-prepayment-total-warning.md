### Per-event FX rates for purchasing: a prepayment that does not match the order's total is reported
- Still behind `purchase_event_fx_rates_enabled` (off) and its interlock (still off); with the switch off nothing changes.
- When a Xero supplier prepayment is stored as an order's exchange-rate lock, IMS compares its total with what the order says is owed to the supplier (its foreign total with additional costs, less self-accounted reverse-charge VAT on goods lines and vatable additional costs), as the order stands when the prepayment is stored. A difference of more than one minor unit of the currency (0.01 GBP, 1 JPY) writes one WARNING activity entry (`xero_prepayment_total_mismatch`, with both totals and the difference) and a console warning. A re-read of the same prepayment does not warn again (a changed total does).
- Advisory only: the lock, the rate and every receipt are unchanged. A deposit or part payment is reported too.
