### Stock value hidden from roles without inventory.prices (security)
- Only roles holding `inventory.prices` (Admin, Manager, Finance) now receive stock value and unit cost on the stock reports, matching Slow Stock. Stock on Hand, Inventory Aging and Dead Stock, and the Stock Movement Ledger, Stock Adjustments, Stock Transfers and Stock Counts reports, no longer send value fields to Warehouse and Read-only users: the pages, summary tiles, totals, the minimum-value filter and the CSV exports omit them on the server, not just in the UI.
- Dead Stock is ordered by SKU instead of value, and the Stock Adjustments top reasons and SKUs are ranked by count, for roles without the permission, so the order cannot reveal value.
- The product list `COGS Value` column and product CSV `inventoryValue`, a product page's COGS value, average unit cost and FIFO cost layers, and the stock-levels export `unitCostBase` and `inventoryValueBase` columns follow the same rule.
- Admin, Manager and Finance see exactly what they saw before.
