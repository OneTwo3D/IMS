### CRM foundation: customer identity, company grouping, WooCommerce customer id per order, customer timeline
- **One person across records.** Customer records whose emails match after folding case, spaces, `+tag` and Gmail/Googlemail dots are shown as one person on the customer page. Nothing is merged or deleted: the originals stay, joins are computed on read, and every automatic join is audited in the new insert-only `customer_merge_events` table. Two different WooCommerce customer ids, an anonymised record or a placeholder email never join. MANAGER and ADMIN can merge two records or separate one (audited); the latest decision for a pair wins.
- **Company grouping.** The customer page lists other customers with the same billing company name or company email domain (free-mail domains never count). A view only.
- **Customer timeline.** Orders, cancellations, invoices, payments, credit notes and refunds, refund payments, expected and received returns for the person or the single record, read-only, newest first, paged. Visible to every sales user.
- **WooCommerce customer id per order.** New nullable `sales_orders.external_customer_id`, filled by the order importer on create and on later deliveries (a positive id only, never on an erased order). Cleared by an erasure. No backfill in the migration; `npm run crm:backfill-order-customer-id` (dry run unless `--apply`) fills it from deliveries already stored, with no WooCommerce call and without raising `updatedAt`.
- **Importer email match is case-insensitive** (and ignores surrounding spaces), so a different capitalisation no longer creates a second customer.
- **Erasure covers the whole person.** Anonymising a customer erases every live record in its cluster, each by the existing per-customer erasure with its own record. A partial failure is reported and can be re-run.
- `npm run crm:identity-report` now also reports how many clusters the rule forms and lists a sample of them as customer ids only. Read-only; the owner has approved running it on production.
- Migrations (additive): `20261119131000_crm_customer_identity` (column, two enums, audit table with insert-only triggers), `20261119131100_sales_orders_customer_created_idx` and `20261119131200_customers_identity_key_idx` (both `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY`).
