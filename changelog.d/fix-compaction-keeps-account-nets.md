### Retention compaction keeps the per-account net the Allocated Inventory reversal proof needs (backend / accounting)

- **Fixed: an old Group A2 journal no longer becomes permanently unprovable.** Retention compacts an aged accounting sync row by dropping its payload, and the proof that an A2 debit reached Allocated Inventory (used before any automatic reversal or refund credit) failed closed on a row with no lines, so no automatic credit could ever be raised against the orders it carried and the pounds waited for a hand-posted credit. The proof asks only for the net movement of one account in one direction, so compaction now writes that net per account (new nullable JSON column `payload_account_nets` on `accounting_sync_logs`, account codes and two totals each, nothing personal) in the same statement that clears the payload, for the journal row types the proof and refund relief read, and the proof reads it once the lines are gone. The figure is the one the full payload would give; the proof's checks are unchanged. Additive migration `20261117110000`.
- **A journal whose payload cannot be read is no longer compacted.** It keeps its payload and is reported in the Data retention activity entry and the run result.
- **Rows compacted before this change stay unprovable** (no payload, no summary) and the proof still refuses them, as before. There is no backfill: production is unused and the payload is gone. Help documents: Xero Sync, Settings.
