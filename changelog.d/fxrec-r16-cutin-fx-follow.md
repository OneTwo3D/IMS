### Reconciliation pack R16 and unrealised FX: follow-ups (accounting)
- R16 now holds each posted storefront receipt and invoice to the ONE date convention it was posted under: the UTC day for a posting created before a recorded London-day cut-in, the London day on or after it (previously both were accepted for every receipt, so a wrong rate on a boundary case could not be detected). The invoice fallback (order instant unknown) cuts "the rates of the day" by the same convention. The cut-in is a system-managed setting (`xero_posted_london_day_cutin_at`) stamped once by migration `20261120090000_xero_posted_london_day_cutin` (additive, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`): no manual step; on a fresh install it precedes every posting, so everything is post-cut-in. A malformed cut-in or creation instant is an `IMS_POSTING_UNREADABLE` finding.
- Unrealised FX: receivables are now the orders whose invoice has been posted to the ledger (PENDING_PAYMENT and ON_HOLD orders with no posted invoice are no longer revalued), and a receivable nets only refunds whose credit note has reached the ledger, the same POSTED basis the payable side already used, because the journal revalues the ledger's own balance. The refund-status filter is gone from the population: a fully refunded order is excluded only once its credit note has posted.
- Daily inventory snapshot: a regression test pins the label and `cutoffAt` as independent of the host's time zone (already computed in Europe/London). Historical BST snapshot rows keep their old labels (accepted limit: the label is part of the key and the rows are point-in-time captures; documented in code).
