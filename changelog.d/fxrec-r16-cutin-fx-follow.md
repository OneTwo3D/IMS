### Reconciliation pack R16 and unrealised FX: follow-ups (accounting)
- R16 now holds each posted storefront receipt and invoice to the date convention it was posted under (a receipt's importer rate stamp is judged by its SOURCE invoice's creation instant, and its paymentDate by the `dateConvention: 'london'` that every INVOICE_PAYMENT payload now records, falling back to the row's creation instant for older payloads, so a revived FAILED row is not misjudged; where the two differ the rate either reading wants is accepted):  the UTC day for a posting created before a recorded London-day cut-in, the London day on or after it (previously both were accepted for every receipt, so a wrong rate on a boundary case could not be detected). The invoice fallback (order instant unknown) cuts "the rates of the day" by the same convention. The cut-in is a system-managed setting (`xero_posted_london_day_cutin_at`) stamped once by migration `20261120090000_xero_posted_london_day_cutin` (additive, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`): no manual step; on a fresh install it precedes every posting, so everything is post-cut-in. A malformed cut-in or creation instant is an `IMS_POSTING_UNREADABLE` finding.
- Unrealised FX: receivables are now the orders whose invoice is in the ledger by any recorded route (an IMS-posted invoice, a Xeroom-era invoice number on a WooCommerce order, or an invoice posted by hand and marked handled or asserted by an operator; PENDING_PAYMENT and ON_HOLD orders with none of these are no longer revalued), and a receivable nets only refunds whose credit note has reached the ledger (back-referenced, posted by hand and marked handled, or operator-asserted; an order admitted only through the Xeroom invoice-number arm nets every live refund, because Xeroom owns the credit notes and IMS records none of them), the same POSTED basis the payable side already used, because the journal revalues the ledger's own balance. The refund-status filter is gone from the population: a fully refunded order is excluded only once its credit note has posted.
- Daily inventory snapshot: a regression test pins the label and `cutoffAt` as independent of the host's time zone (already computed in Europe/London). Historical BST snapshot rows keep their old labels (accepted limit: the label is part of the key and the rows are point-in-time captures; documented in code).
