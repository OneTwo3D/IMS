### Reconciliation pack: four P2 fixes before the cut-over (reconciliation)
- R14 sets aside every IMS product that has a parent (the first load's KIT and BOM variations, 16 on the real data) from the storefront walk, as it already did for type VARIANT; before, each was reported as missing from WooCommerce.
- The Qoblex read no longer drops anything unreported. R2 lists every SKU read as 0 on hand (blank total over blank-or-zero cells) and every ghost row dropped; R14 lists the ghost rows, the per-part counts of rows the native map skipped, and the renames applied. A ghost row with a non-zero quantity makes its part inconclusive (new reason QOBLEX_GHOST_STOCK). The stale "native map skips nothing" comments and the map header are corrected.
- The pack takes the first load's `sku-renames` list as an optional manifest input (`skuRenames`, digest-checked, judged by the first load's own rules, now one shared helper `lib/first-load/sku-renames.ts`) and compares renamed SKUs under the SKU IMS holds; a refused list is SKU_RENAMES_REFUSED. Without it each renamed SKU stays a standing R14 and stock discrepancy.
- Docs: docs/installation.md (reason codes, manifest, evidence, renames).
