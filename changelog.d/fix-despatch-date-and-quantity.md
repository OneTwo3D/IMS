### Mintsoft despatch: dated by when the goods left, and held when the units differ (warehouse)
- **Despatch date** (new setting `wms_despatch_date_enabled`, default OFF, system-managed, `npm run settings:set`): when on, a Mintsoft despatch books the shipment (`shippedAt`), its stock movement, its COGS entries and the order flip at Mintsoft's despatch time instead of the time the sweep applied it. One instant everywhere, so stock reports, snapshots and the daily batch (Group B revenue and COGS) agree. Only an exact despatch time is used (a split order uses its latest part; an undated part never inherits another's date). The booking is bounded: a future time, a time over 31 days old, a time inside a period locked in Xero, inside a day that already has an inventory snapshot, or before the stock was allocated or the order existed, is moved to the nearest acceptable instant and the order's activity log records both times and why.
- **Despatched units** (new setting `wms_despatch_quantity_check_enabled`, default OFF): when on, the units Mintsoft despatched are compared per SKU with the units IMS books before anything is shipped. Exact match required; any difference holds the despatch and raises *Despatched units differ from what IMS books* (Serious, under Refused or stalled actions) with both quantities. Not compared: kit orders, merged orders, despatches with no listed lines. No extra Mintsoft reads.
- Help docs: *Despatch date and despatched units* in the warehouse guide.
