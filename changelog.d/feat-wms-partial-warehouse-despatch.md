### Fulfilment

- A warehouse (Mintsoft) despatch on an order split across warehouses now ships only the despatching warehouse's own shipments and leaves the other warehouses' shipments open, instead of refusing the whole despatch (owner ruling). The order is not marked Shipped and no WooCommerce completion is sent until every shipment has shipped; the shipped part's stock, cost and daily accounting batch follow that shipment as normal. This is not counted as a despatch failure by the circuit breaker. Order lines the warehouse despatched that IMS holds only in another warehouse are not shipped and raise a "Despatch covers lines held in another warehouse" item on the exceptions page. Ship the other warehouse's shipment in IMS when it leaves.
- The warehouse is now sent only the lines and quantities IMS allocated to its own warehouse when an order is allocated to more than one warehouse (creates and amendments; shipping and the order VAT go with that part, once). A single-warehouse order is sent exactly as before. A mixed order with nothing allocated to the warehouse is not sent and says why. This changes what is sent to the live warehouse, for mixed-warehouse orders only.
- A partly despatched order now raises a standing "Other warehouse's part is still to ship" item (and, where the warehouse may have been sent lines IMS holds elsewhere, a "Despatch covers lines held in another warehouse" item) instead of only a log entry, and the warehouse is no longer re-read for it once its part is applied. A fully refunded order whose warehouse part has already shipped is not cancelled at the warehouse.
