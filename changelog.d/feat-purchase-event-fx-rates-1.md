### Per-event FX rates for purchasing, step 1 of 3: settings, schema and switch (o3d-hqvrp.51)
- New ADMIN system switch `purchase_event_fx_rates_enabled` (default off, `npm run settings:set`). Nothing reads it yet: with it off, and in this release even on, receipts, bills, cost layers, journals, GR/IR rows and Xero payloads are unchanged. Turning it on is refused while Xero sync is on and the Realised FX Gain/Loss or the Purchase Price Variance account is unmapped.
- New Xero account mapping **Purchase Price Variance** (`xero_purchase_price_variance_account`); it must differ from Stock in Transit, GR/IR and Inventory Asset.
- Migration `20261122150000_purchase_event_fx_rates` (additive, no backfill): nullable `cost_layers.goods_unit_cost_base`, `cost_layers.fx_rate_to_base` and `purchase_receipts.fx_rate_to_base`, reserved for the valuation step.
- Reserved sub-ledger source types: transit `PURCHASE_FX_VARIANCE`, read by the reconciliation pack's R11 transit schedule (its text now lists all eleven source types, including `GRIR_RECLASS`, which it had left out), and GR/IR `BILL_FX_VARIANCE`, a variance outside the balance.
- GR/IR report (Analytics > GR/IR): a new **FX difference** column, also added to the CSV export (`fxVarianceBase`, after `priceVarianceBase`); it is zero until the valuation step. The report now refuses a sub-ledger source type it does not know instead of summing it into the balance.
