### Mintsoft ASN book-in and stock alignment: three audit fixes (backend / sync / UI)

- **New: a partly delivered purchase-order ASN can be closed short.** A partially booked-in ASN (Mintsoft status DISCREPANCY, PARTIALLYBOOKED or BOOKEDIN-PARTIAL) was never closed, so the undelivered quantity stayed blocked for manual receipt and for a new ASN, and align-down stayed off for the SKU. The ASN table on the purchase order now has **Close short** (permission `purchasing.receive`, a reason is required). It is an operator decision, not automatic, because Mintsoft keeps booking against an ASN in those statuses. Closing sets `closedAt` only: nothing is booked, written off or journalled, the received units stay received, and the undelivered quantity becomes outstanding again (receive it by hand or create a new ASN). It refuses an ASN that is not partially booked in, is already closed, belongs to a transfer, or has a booked-in event not yet processed. No Mintsoft call and no warehouse action is needed.
- **Fixed: align-down no longer writes off units the warehouse has already despatched.** Mintsoft lowers its balance when it despatches; IMS lowers on-hand when the dispatch sweep records the shipment, and a lagging sweep let the same delta persist into a second run, so the later dispatch decremented stock again. A new gate holds align-down for units on orders pushed to Mintsoft and not yet shipped in IMS, unless the order's cached Mintsoft status is FRESH and not despatched. Held: despatched status, a dispatch the sweep gave up on, a missing status (the `wms-order-status` cron is off or has not reached the order) or a stale one. Fresh means fetched after the stock reading being acted on, or within 45 minutes (the sweep re-reads at 30 minutes, every 15). The held orders are named in the sync result and the activity log, and the gate is re-checked inside the adjustment lock at the moment of the write. No live Mintsoft read is added. It lasts for as long as the despatch is unrecorded, unlike the one-cycle persistence gate.
- **Fixed: an approved over-receipt now receives the full booked quantity.** Approving `received_over_expected` on a purchase-order ASN used to book only the expected quantity and close the ASN, leaving the excess in Mintsoft stock and in no IMS stock row, cost layer, PO line or journal. The review now shows the quantity approval will book; approval books all of it (stock, a cost layer at the PO unit cost, the PO line `qtyReceived`, the receipt journal), and a later re-check of the same ASN does not raise the same excess again. Transfer ASNs are unchanged (still capped; an excess there has no cost basis).
