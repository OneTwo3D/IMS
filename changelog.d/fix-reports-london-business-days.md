### COGS, turnover and ledger reports on London business days; valuation GL variance fix (inventory)

- **Fixed: the inventory valuation report failed whenever an accounting connector and an inventory account were configured.** It passed the as-of instant (for example `2026-07-01T22:59:59.999Z`) to the ledger balance lookup, which accepts only a `YYYY-MM-DD` day, so the lookup threw. It now passes the London day label, so the stored balance is looked up, and compared with the as-of day, by that label.
- **Changed: the Stock Movement Ledger, COGS Report, Inventory Turnover and Landed Cost Analysis date ranges are London business days.** A From date starts at the end of the previous London day and a To date includes the whole London day (23:00Z to 23:00Z in BST, midnight to midnight in GMT), and their default dates are the London day. With the valuation already on London days, opening valuation (as of the day before From) plus the stock received in the period, minus COGS, now equals closing valuation (as of the To date) across a summer-time month-end; before, movements between 23:00 and 24:00 UTC fell on different days in different reports. GMT results are unchanged. The COGS report's credit (refund) window deliberately stays on the UTC day, because it must match the Gross Margin report's credit window; both move together in a later change.
- **Fixed: a snapshot backfill could mark its result unreliable for no reason.** A trailing reverse pass after the earliest written day counted null-value movements that no written row depends on. It is skipped now; a null-value movement that an earlier day's row does depend on still marks the run unreliable.
- **Docs and wording.** `help-docs/analytics.md` now explains that stock dates and as-of dates name London days, that exports show `22:59:59.999Z` in summer time for a date-only as-of, and that the nightly snapshot is taken at London midnight (it said "00:00 UTC"). The as-of reader's comment and the earlier changelog entry no longer claim that legacy summer-time cron snapshot rows are read correctly: their stored cutoff is 23h before their content end, an accepted limit not corrected here.
