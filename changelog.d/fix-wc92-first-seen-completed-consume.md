### WooCommerce: orders first seen as completed, shipped, delivered or refunded
- An order IMS first sees already completed (or mapped to Shipped / Delivered) on or after the instant the opening stock was taken is created Processing and despatched through the existing external-fulfilment path: on-hand goes down, FIFO layers are consumed, the cost of sales is posted and the order becomes Shipped, with nothing sent to a warehouse. One completed before that instant (the first reconciliation walks the whole store history), or with no instant on record, is recorded with no allocation, movement or cost.
- The first-load manifest has a new required `stockExtractedAt` (ISO UTC, the time the Qoblex stock was read; digest-bound; refused if missing, malformed, after the apply or over 45 days old). The first-load apply records it once as `facts.openingStockCutoffAt`; the order import uses it as the cut-off. Existing manifests with an opening-stock step must add it.
- If stock is short, the import still succeeds (the sweep cursor advances); the owed despatch is listed under Sync > Exceptions and retried by the 15-minute job, capped at 30 attempts / 14 days, ending in a visible terminal state. Partial stock is owed too (the reservation is released, retried as stock arrives).
- An order first seen as refunded stays refundable (Processing, no allocation) so the refund sweep can record its refunds; it is never pushed to a warehouse or allocated by the reallocation or backorder sweeps.
- New `sales_orders.outside_ims_fulfilment_at` (additive migration) marks such orders; the warehouse create fences and both allocation sweeps read it.
