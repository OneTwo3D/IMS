### Sales credit notes: what must hold before the two credit-note switches are turned on (both stay off)
- Credit-note settlement (`xero_credit_note_allocation_enabled`): before a credit note is allocated or refunded, IMS reads Xero (read only) for the order's other sales credit notes, by the WooCommerce order id, by every refund id of the order and by `SC-<refund id>`. Cash paid out on credit notes IMS did not raise (Xeroom-era, or by hand) now counts against the refund cap, so 40 + 40 can no longer go out against 50 received. A credit note IMS did not raise that credits the same refund holds the settlement (nothing is credited twice); allocations to a replacement order's invoice are not counted as cash; a voided (combined-order) invoice is never settled against.
- The order-locked re-checks of a refund payment count only cash that is out or may be; another refund that has merely claimed cash no longer refuses a row after its attempt was stamped. A younger refund waits for an older one that has claimed cash but not made its call, rather than being settled short for good. The order lock's loss is checked before the call, and AmountPaid is read again under the lock.
- A refund payment held because Xero shows the receipt is not in yet (or AmountPaid cannot be read), or because another refund of the order is posting, now waits (15 minutes, 1 minute) without using up its retries, instead of failing after five tries.
- The stated limit on AmountPaid is corrected: it is cash receipts only (applied credit is AmountCredited).
- Before IMS creates a sales credit note, new system switch `xero_credit_note_duplicate_check_enabled` (default off) makes the same read-only search and holds the row instead of creating a duplicate.
- Refund parity (`wc_refund_credit_note_parity_enabled`): a parity hold on Sync exceptions can now be closed with **Settled outside IMS** (creates nothing; who and why are recorded). Open fee repayments are their own list on Sync exceptions, cleared only by **Repayment applied**, as the storefront plugin keeps them. A historic order's fee is recognised from the original order item's `_wclc_fee`. Only refunds made earlier count as earlier; VAT-only refunds are held; store-credit coupons are looked up by creation time, oldest first, and the walk no longer stops on a short page; a store-credit refund with no creation time is held with that reason; the WooCommerce order is read only when needed.
- The retry guard's copy of the live-unique index covers `BILL_PAYMENT` and `CREDIT_NOTE_ALLOCATION` again, checked against the latest migration that defines it.
- Migration `20261122002000_refund_fee_repayment_applied` (additive: three nullable columns on `sales_order_refunds`).
- Operator checklist (read-only checks of the WooCommerce coupons endpoint, refund 166128's payload and the Xero credit-note search) in docs/installation.md, to be made by hand before either switch is turned on.
