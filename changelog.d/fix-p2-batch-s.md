### Invoice PDF VAT detail and London-day document dates

- The IMS-rendered invoice (download and email attachment) now shows each line's VAT rate, and prints reverse-charge wording under the totals when a line's tax rate is flagged reverse charge. The seller VAT number was already printed.
- The date sent to the accounting system on sales invoices (manual and WooCommerce import), credit notes, the refund COGS and allocation reversal journals and the realised FX journal is now the Europe/London calendar day of the moment, not the UTC day, so an order at 00:30 BST on 1 July is dated 1 July (not 30 June, the previous VAT quarter). Documents already posted keep the date they were posted with; only the date derivation changed.
- One order's legs now agree on the day: the payment registered with a WooCommerce invoice, a manual payment's registered date, and the daily batch's revenue deferral, inventory reclassification and recognition (which read the day the order was paid, and the run's own day) all use the London day as well. A WooCommerce invoice held before this change, whose date is the UTC day of an order created 23:00-23:59 UTC in summer time, is no longer refused as "order changed": the parked date is accepted and released as parked.
- The same London day now runs through every remaining accounting-date producer and checker: the refund-delete counter journal, its COGS sub-ledger row and the credit-note void (never dated before what they counter), the return-receipt Stock-in-Transit clearing journal, the receipt matcher's lock-date and outbound-hold check, the storefront receipt's "paid on the order's day" rate test (importer and reconciliation pack R16: the payment-day rate is read as of the end of the payment's London day), the daily batch's shipment, allocation and refund event days, and the purchase receipt, supplier return and credit note, stock adjustment, landed-cost, shipment COGS revaluation, manufacturing, allocation-reversal, WMS and Mintsoft receipt journals. Keys and stored back-references are unchanged; documents already queued or posted keep their date.
