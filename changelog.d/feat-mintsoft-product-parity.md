### Mintsoft product sync parity with the retired Python sync (flags, default OFF)

- **Added: the setting `mintsoft_product_parity_enabled`, default off.** Behind it: product names fitted to Mintsoft's 99-character limit (`fit_mintsoft_name` ported exactly: parent cut at a word boundary with `...`, variation suffix kept whole, UTF-16 counting; golden tests run the Python function), the IMS unit cost sent as `CostPrice` (weighted average of cost layers, Decimal, four places; kept out of every log and out of the sync-log echo), and a variation inheriting its parent's weight and dimensions per missing field. With it off the product payload, its hash and the connector options are byte-identical to before; a test pins this. It is not live.
- **Added: the setting `mintsoft_parent_sku_delete_enabled`, default off (inert unless the parity setting is on).** After a successful full product verify run IMS may delete a variable product's parent SKU in Mintsoft (new write operation `mintsoft/product.delete`, producer-seam and hold governed), only on proof from a fresh read of the stored links that every variation is present; otherwise it deletes nothing and records a refused action for an operator. Fail-closed tests cover each refusal.
- **Added: read-only and dry-run-by-default tools.** `scripts/mintsoft-catalogue-dry-run.ts` diffs what IMS would send with the stored Mintsoft record (no write, no network); `scripts/mintsoft-bulk-product-data.ts` ports the trade-data and country-of-origin CSV pushes (dry run unless `--apply` and the parity setting; apply writes IMS products only). Docs: Mintsoft connector and Warehouse help.
