### Reorder Planning: per-product demand chart (UI / analytics)

- **New: a read-only demand chart for each Reorder Planning row.** A **Chart** icon at the end of every row opens `/analytics/reorder/<product>`, carrying the row's warehouse, category, supplier, product type, demand window and weeks of supply. It shows weekly units sold over the demand window, flat-average projected demand, dated open PO arrivals, the projected available-stock line, safety stock and reorder point lines, and markers for Required receipt, Latest PO date, First shortage and today. The required receipt quantity is plotted separately from existing POs. No sales in the window shows a message instead of a chart; a text summary and data table accompany the chart. Gated like Reorder Planning (Admin, Manager, Finance); no migration. The historical stock line and stockout shading wait on the stockout-aware demand work. Help document: Analytics.
