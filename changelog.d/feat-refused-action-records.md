### Refused or stalled actions are recorded, aged and escalated (zvec.10, zvec.8, split orders)

- **Added: one durable record of an action a check refused or stalled** (`refused_actions`): the entity, the action requested, the gate that refused it, whether it is permanent or retryable, a severity taken from what was being changed, an intent summary with no personal data, first seen, last seen and resolved. It is an upsert on (entity, action, gate): a repeat refusal moves last seen and a counter and never makes a second row, also under concurrent writers. A later success, or the order no longer needing the action, closes it.
- **Added: age-based escalation.** The existing WMS watchdog cron alerts the admins once per record when a refusal has stood past its severity's age: serious 2 hours, medium 24 hours, low impact never. The ages are named constants in `lib/domain/refused-actions.ts` (the codebase tunes no alert age from settings).
- **Fixed: a Mintsoft amendment attempted after the order left NEW is no longer abandoned silently** (o3d-zvec.8). The link still stays synced with `lastError` set and `pushedAt` moved (IMS does not hammer Mintsoft), but the refusal is now recorded with the full push intent and the Mintsoft status. Severity comes from the intent: a SKU or quantity change (or one that cannot be ruled out) is serious and adds a **Raise a warehouse query** task with the exact lines (new reason `AMENDMENT_NOT_APPLIED`); a note, VAT or price change is low impact. If the record cannot be written the link stays due. IMS writes no Mintsoft order comment.
- **Fixed: a split order with a cancelled part** no longer reads as "N/M parts despatched" for ever. It is recorded as a stalled action (medium) and its own outcome, kept out of the connector-drift cohort and not holding the watermark; bounded by a per-link read count that sets the order aside after 5 reads. It is never dead-lettered.
- **Fixed: marking the warehouse-query task Done does not close the refusal.** It closes when the push sweep reads the Mintsoft order's lines back and they match the intent, when the order ends, or when an amendment later succeeds.
- **Added: a "Refused or stalled actions" category on the Needs attention card, and a section on Sync > Exceptions,** and a count in the banner and total for serious and medium rows.
- **Migrations:** `20261117050000_refused_action_records` (one new table and two enums) and `20261117050100_wms_amendment_task_reason` (one value on the operator-task reason enum). Both additive. The table is classified runtime state for the first load and not personal for erasure.
