### First-load tool
- Fixed: `LIFECYCLE_DISAGREES_WITH_WOO` now fires on Qoblex data. Every joined VARIANT, KIT or BOM variation is compared with its WooCommerce parent's status; a DRAFT (Archived) variation under a published parent is a blocking ERROR (a DRAFT product forces a WooCommerce draft and could unpublish a live listing) unless the SKU is confirmed on the new `lifecycle-confirmations` decision list (`sku,reason`). Other differences are warnings. `woo-products` gains an optional `status` column: a DRAFT simple, KIT or BOM product against a published listing is blocked the same way, and DRAFT products without a usable status are listed as `DRAFT_PRODUCT_WOO_STATUS_UNKNOWN`.
- Fixed: `non-stock-skus` fills the kind only when the source cell is blank, `Unknown` or non-inventory; a SKU whose source states another type (simple, variable, kit, bom, ...) is refused as `NON_STOCK_DECISION_CONFLICT` instead of losing its stock silently. A nonzero quantity ignored on a non-stock SKU is reported as INFO `NON_STOCK_QTY_IGNORED`.
- Fixed: `sku-renames` refuses a rename whose `fromSku` or `toSku` is on `sku-exclusions` (`RENAME_FROM_EXCLUDED` / `RENAME_TO_EXCLUDED`) instead of silently loading an excluded product.
