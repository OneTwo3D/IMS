### WooCommerce order edits: unpaid-order rewrite, paid-order increase task, recheck drain fixes (woocommerce)

- **New switch `wc_order_amendment_unpaid_rewrite_enabled` (off by default; Sync > Orders, and `npm run settings:set`).** An UNPAID order edited in WooCommerce before the warehouse picks it is rewritten in IMS to exactly WooCommerce's lines and totals: WooCommerce's own line totals, tax, shipping, discount and order total are stored as stated, never re-derived or re-priced (only base amounts are converted, at the order's booked rate). Continued lines keep their row, removed lines are deleted, added lines created, stock released and re-reserved, and the order's `updatedAt` moves so the warehouse amend sweep picks the change up. At or after picking IMS rewrites nothing and raises the `WC_EDIT_AFTER_PICKING` operator task instead. It refuses (a Sync > Exceptions refused action, nothing written) on anything it cannot map exactly: an unknown SKU, a tax or total mismatch, a currency difference, store credit, fee lines, an order edited in IMS, or an order that already has an invoice, shipment, refund or return. It never touches a paid order and nothing is ever written back to WooCommerce (WooCommerce stays authoritative). Needs `wc_order_divergence_detection_enabled`. No migration.
- **Paid-order increases raise a task (behind `wc_order_amendment_write_enabled`).** A quantity up, an added item or a higher unit price on a PAID order changes nothing in IMS; an operator task names the lines and the order-total difference (a refused action where no warehouse order exists to carry it).
- **Recheck drain (o3d-hu8g9).** A deferred comparison that ages out at 14 days with its latest live reads still failing (or queued by the write step and never read) now raises the refused action instead of being dropped silently; the refused-action wording no longer says "after 0 live re-reads" for failed reads; a row deleted or re-created during its live read no longer aborts the rest of the drain (updateMany, not update). Help text corrected (the drain runs when the order sweep saves its position, and it also applies with the report alone on).
