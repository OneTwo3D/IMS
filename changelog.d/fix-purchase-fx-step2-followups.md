### Per-event FX rates for purchasing: step 2 review follow-ups (o3d-lwr6b)
- Still behind `purchase_event_fx_rates_enabled` (off by default); with it off nothing changes. Turning it on is now **refused until step 3 is installed** (an interlock constant that step 3 flips), whatever the account mappings.
- An unused locked rate is re-read from Xero once before the first receipt and replaced or removed under the order lock, so a prepayment voided and re-entered in Xero is not frozen at the stale rate. The lazy first lock is also taken under the order lock (no race with the on-demand read).
- A "no prepayment" answer is remembered per order for 6 hours (`purchase_fx_no_prepayment_ttl_hours`, 0 = off), and the Mintsoft align-up pre-reads only orders with capacity left, protecting the Xero budget shared with other integrations. Migration `20261126120000_purchase_order_fx_prepayment_cache` (additive: `purchase_orders.fx_no_prepayment_checked_at`).
- Refusals are per order: an alignment or book-in refuses only for an order it really values. A warehouse booking-in refused only because Xero could not be read, its budget is spent, a table rate is missing or an account is unmapped now waits 30 minutes without using its retry attempts; real ambiguity still counts.
- A receipt that falls back to the table rate while Xero sync is on shows a warning and writes an activity entry. A prepayment recorded after earlier receipts applies to later receipts only; the order page lists the rate every receipt used.
- An unlinked supplier is matched to its Xero contact through a read-only lookup (email, then exact name) and the link stored when it is the prepayment's contact; the refusal message names the real remedy.
- While the switch is on, Xero readiness lists Realised FX Gain/Loss and Purchase Price Variance as required mappings. The lock is marked used only when goods are laid at its rate; a retried receipt with a committed idempotency token no longer asks Xero; a refusal before the receipt is logged.
