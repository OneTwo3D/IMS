### Mintsoft despatch switches: prerequisites fixed before either may be turned on (warehouse)
- **Snapshot race closed.** The daily inventory snapshot now holds an advisory lock from before it reads its state to the commit of its run row, and a backdated despatch holds the same lock (shared) while it reads the snapshot floor, so a despatch can no longer commit between the two and go missing from the snapshot for good.
- **Floors.** A backdated despatch is never dated before the receipt of the FIFO layer it consumes (COGS, movement, shipment and order flip move to that receipt). A stored Xero lock date that is unparseable or was last read more than 24 hours ago is not trusted: the despatch is booked at the apply time instead. No stored value at all is an explicit "no lock".
- **`wms_despatch_date_enabled` needs the Inventory Snapshot job.** With the job off (its default) the switch has no effect, and the readiness report warns (`wms-despatch-date:snapshot-cron-disabled`).
- **A held despatch stays held.** A status read without item rows (the 30-minute reconcile, the delta fallback) no longer applies a despatch held on a units mismatch or closes its standing row; only a despatch closes it. A neutral pass no longer closes and reopens the row (which restarted its two-hour alert). The hold WARNING is logged once per change, not on every pass (about 96 a day).
- **Despatched units, stated accurately.** Mintsoft's `OrderItems.Quantity` is the order line's quantity, not a count of units picked: the check catches orders Mintsoft holds differently from IMS (an amendment refused after New, a line IMS no longer books), not warehouse over-picks or substitutions. A SKU Mintsoft sent that IMS books nothing of (a fully refunded line) is a mismatch of N against 0; NON_INVENTORY and VARIABLE lines are ignored. The kit exclusion follows the line's pinned fulfilment recipe rather than the product's current type.
- **Ordering.** The units are compared before auto-allocation and shipment creation, before any partial shipment of a split order is pushed to the storefront, and again under the order lock before any stock moves.
- **Exits documented.** The refusal text and help say what to do when Mintsoft has more (ship by hand, then a stock adjustment) or fewer (reduce the line, ship by hand). `docs/mintsoft.md` lists the three-order stage check (despatched, split, amended after New) that must pass before either switch is enabled.
