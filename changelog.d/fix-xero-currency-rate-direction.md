### Xero CurrencyRate is sent as IMS's rate, not its inverse (Xero, multi-currency)

- **Fixed (go-live blocker, never exercised):** every foreign invoice, bill, credit note, supplier credit note, payment, bill payment and credit-note refund payment IMS sends to Xero now carries `CurrencyRate` = the IMS rate as is (`fxRateToBase` / `settlementRateToBase`, rounded to Xero's 6 decimal places). Xero's `CurrencyRate` is units of the foreign currency per one unit of the base currency, the same convention IMS stores; the connector used to send `1 / rate`, which would have booked a USD 1,000 bill at 1.3125 as GBP 1,312.50 instead of GBP 761.90. Confirmed read-only against the live organisation's own foreign bills (USD 1.352650993, EUR 1.1835295458, CNY 8.937375334, all foreign per GBP). No foreign document or payment had been sent from IMS yet (no sync row with a rate other than 1), so nothing in Xero needs correcting and there is no backfill. Not behind a switch: a switch defaulting to the wrong rate has no use.
- A rate of 10^12 or more (too large for Xero's `Decimal(18,6)`) or one that rounds to zero at 6 places is refused like a missing rate (`foreign_document_missing_rate` / `foreign_payment_missing_rate`), never sent.
- **Reconciliation pack:** R12 now puts a foreign Xero invoice in GBP by DIVIDING by Xero's `CurrencyRate` (it multiplied), and R16 compares Xero's `CurrencyRate` with the IMS rate itself (it compared the reciprocal). The R12 and R16 text in `docs/installation.md` follows.
