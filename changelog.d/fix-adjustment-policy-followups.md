### Stock adjustment reason/cost follow-ups, before the switch goes on (inventory)
All of this applies only with `stock_adjustment_reason_cost_required` ON; with it off every path is unchanged (the one new read is the switch itself).
- In-place edit of an adjustment: only a plain stock adjustment can be edited (a supplier return, stock-count variance or Mintsoft align-down is refused); and while accounting posting applies, an edit that changes the movement's value to the penny is refused with a message to reverse and re-enter it (previously +5 at 0 edited to -5 booked a valued removal with no journal and no inbox row).
- Mintsoft align-down: an unmapped inventory account now holds the delta as a manual discrepancy (named in the hold reason) instead of a per-SKU sweep error on every run.
- The default cost of a blank addition now reads only purchase-receipt and production-output layers (not transfer-recreated, refund-restock, adjustment or opening layers), and ties on the received time are broken by a new creation sequence on cost layers (additive migration `cost_layers.created_seq`), not the cuid.
- The journal is valued from the 6dp movement total (no one-penny mismatch); the refusal for a retired accounting chart no longer says to map an account; the adjustments export gains a `reason` column; the default-unit-cost lookup states its 200-pair cap and the bulk dialog asks in chunks.
- Not changed (documented on the bead): CSV preview not applying the cost default/posting refusal, the "Stock count" seed-name ambiguity, WooCommerce import driver passing no reason, 0.0000004 rounding to 0 silently, the form's render-time default, the doubled "Stock count: Stock count" note, and the supplier-return writer creating reasonless adjustment movements.
