### PO minimum margin (warn or block) and editing the sales price from the margin column (purchasing / settings)

- **New: a minimum gross margin for purchase order lines.** Settings > Purchasing > Minimum Margin takes a global minimum %, optional per-category and per-product overrides (the most specific wins: product, then parent product, then category, then global) and a mode. **Warn** marks a line below its minimum on the PO form. **Block** makes the server refuse to save or confirm-and-send the order while any line is below its minimum, unless an override note is entered; an accepted override is recorded in the order's activity (note, lines, who, when) in the same transaction. A margin that cannot be computed is shown as unknown and is never blocked. With no minimum set, nothing changes. The check recomputes each line on the server from the cost it is about to store, in decimal; it never trusts the browser. Stored in the settings table (`po_min_margin_threshold`); no migration.
- **New: edit a product's sales price from the PO margin column** (needs `inventory.prices`). The ex-VAT price entered is converted to the product's stored convention (VAT-inclusive or not, same rule as the margin), written through the product update mechanics (SKU write lock, one transaction, activity row with old and new price), and reaches WooCommerce and Mintsoft only through the normal product sync. A variation is priced on its own row; a variable parent is refused. The margin is recomputed afterwards.
- Internal only, like the margin: nothing reaches the supplier portal, PDF or email. Help documents: Purchasing, Settings.
