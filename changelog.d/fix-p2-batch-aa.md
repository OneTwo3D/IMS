### Fulfilment and WooCommerce completion fixes

- A warehouse (Mintsoft) despatch no longer drives a shipment IMS holds in a different warehouse to Shipped. If IMS has an unshipped shipment for the order outside the warehouse that serves the WMS, the despatch is refused (nothing is shipped), logged, and shows under Sync > Exceptions as a stuck despatch; reallocate the order to the WMS warehouse or ship the other warehouse by hand, then replay it.
- The WooCommerce completion job re-checks that the IMS order is still Shipped (or Completed/Delivered) on every attempt. An order reopened or un-shipped while the job was waiting is dropped without sending tracking or a completed status to WooCommerce.
- The `product.updated` webhook now follows the product sync settings, as the hourly poll does: it imports into IMS only when product sync is on and the direction is "WooCommerce to IMS" or "Both". With sync off, or IMS as the product master ("IMS to WooCommerce"), the delivery is acknowledged, logged and not imported. The stock correction on the same webhook is unchanged.
- After a warehouse despatch is recorded, IMS compares the quantities the warehouse reports with what it booked, per SKU. A difference opens a `DESPATCH_QTY_MISMATCH` finding under Sync > Exceptions (order-level drift) and notifies administrators. IMS changes nothing it booked.
