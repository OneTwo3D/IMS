### Xero daily batch: COGS is booked when the goods ship, paid or not (accounting / sync)

- **Fixed: an order shipped before it was paid posted no COGS until payment, and none at all if the debt was written off.** The daily batch now posts a shipment's cost when the goods leave. For an order that Group A1 has not deferred yet (unpaid, or paid and waiting for its first run) and that IMS has invoiced, the shipment is costed on its own journal, dated the day it shipped: DR COGS / CR Inventory, at the same FIFO cost as the ordinary path. Allocated Inventory is not touched, because Group A2 never reclassified that stock. The invoice already booked the revenue, so the journal has no revenue leg; the shipment only records its revenue share. A written-off order keeps its cost.
- **Changed: payment-time steps skip units that are already costed.** The shipment is marked in the same statement as its journal stamp (new nullable columns `accounting_cogs_relieved_from_inventory_at`, `..._amount` and `..._sync_log_id` on `shipments`; additive, no backfill). When the order is paid, Group A1 defers only the part that has not shipped (the order still stores the full deferral, so Group B's true-up and the refund balances stay consistent), Group A2 reclassifies only the units still to ship, and Group B never costs those units again. A refund that returns stock from such a shipment reverses its cost DR Inventory / CR COGS, never into Allocated Inventory, including a refund on an order that is still unpaid. The missing-batch rebuild credits Inventory for these shipments and posts no revenue for them, the accounting checks no longer report them as posted without payment or staging, and the daily batch preview leaves them out of the A2 figure. Ordinary (paid) orders are unchanged.
- **Fixed (same change): a refund entered before payment of goods that already shipped no longer over-adjusts Unearned Revenue, and a failed COGS reversal for such a refund is re-staged on retry.** Group A1 defers only the unshipped part, so a pre-batch refund is now adjusted against Unearned Revenue only for the part that came out of deferred revenue (unshipped goods are refunded first); the part for shipped goods is reversed by the credit note alone. The order stores the deferral plus that shipped part, so the shipping true-up and the refund balances still match the ledger, and a rebuilt journal uses the same split. If staging of the COGS reversal for a refund on a not-yet-deferred order fails, the accounting retry now stages it again (once) instead of reporting success with nothing queued.
