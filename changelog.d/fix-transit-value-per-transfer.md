### Inventory snapshot and GL reconciliation: stock in transit valued per transfer (accounting)
- Stock on a dispatched, not fully received transfer is no longer missing from the inventory sub-ledger. The daily snapshot (and the snapshot backfill) now records an **in transit** line, one row per transfer, and the Inventory GL reconciliation compares the GL with the on-hand value plus that line. Before, the value showed as a gap between dispatch and receipt.
- The value is measured transfer by transfer: cost dispatched, plus landed-cost revaluations capitalised for the units while in transit, minus the cost received. Every receipt route counts: manual and partial receipts, the cancel-dispatch restore, the Mintsoft booked-in webhook and the Mintsoft stock-sync alignment credit. It is exact as of any past instant, so a transfer dispatched before a snapshot day and received after it is in transit on that day. This replaces the global TRANSFER_OUT minus TRANSFER_IN netting that was backed out of #1027, which never cleared for Mintsoft receipts and drifted after revaluations.
- New additive tables `inventory_transit_snapshots` and `stock_transfer_transit_revaluations`, and `inventory_snapshot_runs.inTransitCovered`. Landed-cost revaluations of in-transit units are now mirrored into `stock_transfer_transit_revaluations` in the same transaction as their journal amount; ones made before this release are not (see help-docs/xero-sync.md).
