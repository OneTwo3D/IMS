### Merge candidates: flag same-customer orders still NEW in Mintsoft (flag mode, default OFF)

- **Added: the setting `wms_merge_candidate_mode`, default off** (Integrations > Mintsoft > Merge candidates). Values `off` and `flag`; anything else, including the reserved `hold`, reads as off. With it off nothing is computed and nothing is written. It is not live.
- **Added: detection of a customer's open orders that are all still NEW in Mintsoft.** Same normalised billing email and delivery address (street, line 2, city, postcode, country; case, spacing and punctuation ignored; billing address when there is no shipping address), read from the stored status snapshots with no Mintsoft call. Backordered orders and orders with a pending withdrawal are left out; NI, JE, GG and IM count as UK; EU groups carry a customs / IOSS warning and FedEx groups a "label by hand" warning; chains of three or more are one group; orders Mintsoft already merged count once.
- **Added: what a person sees.** A Mergeable badge with links to the other orders on the sales order, an activity-log entry, a list on the Mintsoft screen, and ONE operator task per group (reason `MERGE_CANDIDATE`, Sync > Exceptions > Raise a warehouse query): check the orders in Mintsoft and merge them there if appropriate. The task is resolved by IMS when the group stops qualifying. No Mintsoft or WooCommerce order note is written, because warehouse staff do not read notes.
- **IMS never merges.** Mintsoft has no merge API; merging stays manual in the Mintsoft UI. Hold mode (holding the orders in Mintsoft until they are merged) is not built.
- **Migration:** adds one value to the operator-task reason enum (additive; no table or row is touched).
