### Reorder Planning: forecast sales and margin columns, and a growth factor (analytics)

- **New: Forecast sales and Forecast margin beside Suggested qty.** Forecast quantity (daily demand over the weeks of supply) at the product's list price ex-VAT, and that less its current unit cost, in the base currency. A VAT-inclusive list price has the sales VAT rate taken off, as in the purchase order margin preview. A product with no cost layers (or no usable list price) shows "unavailable" for margin rather than 0 or 100%. They are forward-looking estimates, not booked sales. Shown to roles holding the price permission, and appended as the last two CSV columns so existing columns keep their positions. Help document: Analytics.
- **New: Growth % on Reorder Planning (default empty/0, range -90 to +500, validated on the server).** Scales the calculated daily demand before everything is derived from it, so the suggested quantity, reorder point, Required receipt and Latest PO dates, forecast figures, the demand chart's projected weeks and the draft PO and production order buttons all agree. Not saved: entered each time, carried through the page address, the CSV and the chart. At 0 reports, CSVs and drafts are unchanged. No migration.
