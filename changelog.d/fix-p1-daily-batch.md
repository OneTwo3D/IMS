### Xero daily batch: journals dated by event, pre-batch refunds netted (accounting / sync)

- **Fixed: every daily-batch journal was dated the day the batch ran.** An order paid, allocated and shipped on 31 March was deferred, reclassified, recognised and costed on 1 April, overstating March margin and leaving the inventory and COGS reconciliations comparing event-dated subledger rows with run-dated journals. Revenue deferral is now dated the day the order was paid, the inventory reclassification the later of paid and allocated, and shipment revenue and COGS the later of shipped and paid. A run raises one journal (with its own reference) per date; the COGS subledger row carries its journal's date. Re-running posts nothing twice.
- **New: the Xero books lock date is imported and respected.** IMS reads the period lock date and year-end lock date from Xero's Organisation endpoint (read-only, existing `accounting.settings` permission; the later date wins) at the start of each daily batch run and when the chart of accounts is synced, and shows it read-only on the Xero settings screen. An event on or before it is never back-dated: its journal is dated the first open day (the day after the lock), says so in its narration, and logs a warning; if that day is still in the future, nothing is staged for those events this run and a warning says they wait. If the read fails the last stored date is used (with a warning); if none was ever read, journals are dated by event.
- **Fixed: a partial refund entered before an order's first batch run was never netted.** The deferral was subtotal + shipping - discount whatever had been refunded, so Unearned Revenue kept a credit for ever and Sales was understated at shipment. The order's stored deferral is netted of refunds recorded before the deferral, but the paid-day journal stays at the full amount and each such refund is posted as its own adjustment dated the refund day (never changing an earlier period), and the deferral lifecycle tie-out now covers such orders. Each such refund records its adjustment journal (new nullable `deferral_adjustment_ref` column, additive, no backfill), so a lost adjustment is rebuilt once, and a paid-day journal is rebuilt at the gross amount only for stamps written by this path (older stamps rebuild at their stored amount). Failed-journal requeues, missing-batch rebuilds and the reconciliation sweeps skip any journal dated on or before the Xero lock date, with a warning.
- Not changed here (design note on the issue): COGS for an order shipped before payment is still held until payment.
